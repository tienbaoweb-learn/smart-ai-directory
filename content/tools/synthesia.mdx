## Overview

Synthesia turns a written script into a video featuring a realistic AI avatar presenter, removing the need for a camera, studio, or on-screen talent to produce professional-looking video content. Users write or paste a script, choose an avatar and language, and Synthesia generates a finished video — making video production accessible to teams without dedicated production resources.

For architecture, construction, and real estate businesses that regularly need video — client updates, training content, listing introductions — but don't have an in-house video team, this removes one of the biggest practical barriers: the time and cost of traditional filming.

This review evaluates Synthesia based on its avatar realism, script-to-video workflow, and practical fit for recurring business video needs heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Script-to-Video Generation

Synthesia converts a written script directly into a finished video with an AI avatar presenting it, collapsing what would normally require filming, editing, and voice recording into a single, fast workflow.

### Wide Avatar & Language Library

The platform offers a large library of AI avatars along with support for many languages and accents, making it practical to produce localized versions of the same content without re-filming with different presenters.

### Easy Script-Based Editing

Because the video is generated from a script, updating content is as simple as editing the text and re-rendering — no need to re-film or re-record because of a changed detail or outdated figure.

### Professional Studio Look Without a Studio

Output videos have a consistent, polished, studio-quality look regardless of where or how the script was written, removing variability that comes from different filming locations, lighting, or equipment.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Turns a written script into a polished, presenter-led video in minutes
- ✅ Wide library of AI avatars and support for many languages and accents
- ✅ Removes the need for camera equipment, studio space, or on-camera talent
- ✅ Easy to update — re-render a video by editing the script rather than reshooting

## Cons

- ❌ AI avatars, while polished, can still feel less personal than a real human presenter
- ❌ Best results require a clear, well-written script — the tool doesn't write strategy for you
- ❌ Higher-tier plans are needed for custom avatars or higher usage volume
- ❌ Not a fit for content needing real, on-location footage rather than a studio presenter

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Synthesia is the right tool if you:

- **Need recurring video content** — training, updates, intros — without ongoing production costs
- **Want to produce videos in multiple languages** without hiring presenters for each
- **Need to update video content frequently** as details or figures change
- **Don't have in-house video production resources** but still want professional results

Synthesia is **not** the right tool if you:

- Need real, on-location footage of an actual property, job site, or project
- Want the personal, human connection of a real presenter rather than an AI avatar
- Are producing content where authenticity of the presenter matters more than polish

---

## Alternatives to Consider

Synthesia is strong for script-to-video avatar content, but it is not the only option:

- **Seedance 2.0** — better for generating cinematic property or product video from photos rather than scripted presenter content
- **Midjourney** — useful for static concept imagery rather than video with a presenter
- **Jasper AI** — relevant for writing the script itself before turning it into a Synthesia video
- **Guideflow** — better for interactive product demos rather than presenter-led video
- **ChatGPT** — useful for drafting scripts, though it doesn't produce video itself

---

## Final Verdict

Synthesia earns its place for businesses that need consistent, professional video content without the recurring cost and scheduling overhead of traditional production. The combination of script-to-video speed, multilingual avatars, and easy script-based updates makes it practical to scale video output beyond what occasional filming would allow.

For content that depends on real footage — an actual property walkthrough or job site — Synthesia isn't the right layer; it shines specifically for scripted, presenter-led communication.

**Our recommendation:** Start with one recurring video need — listing intros or safety training are common starting points — and measure how much production time it saves before expanding video output across your team.
