## Overview

cloro is a search API built for the AI era — one unified endpoint for querying ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, Google AI Mode, Google Search, and Google News, returning real-time structured JSON with markdown, sources, and citations from each platform's response. Beyond raw querying, it provides AI Search Visibility Tracking specifically for monitoring how AI engines mention and cite a brand, calculating mention rate, share of voice, and identifying which sources those AI platforms are actually citing.

For architecture, construction, and real estate businesses, this matters because a growing share of research and vendor discovery now happens through AI assistants rather than a traditional Google search — and traditional SEO tools don't show whether ChatGPT or Perplexity is even aware a business exists, let alone how favorably they describe it.

This review evaluates cloro based on its platform coverage, monitoring depth, and practical fit for businesses wanting visibility into AI-driven discovery heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Unified Multi-Platform Query API

Rather than building separate integrations for each AI platform, cloro provides one API to query ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, Google AI Mode, Google Search, and Google News as agent tools.

### AI Search Visibility Tracking

The platform monitors how AI engines mention and cite a specific brand, calculating mention rate and share of voice, and identifying the citation sources those AI platforms are actually pulling from when they mention a business.

### Structured, Citable Output

Every query returns real-time structured JSON — including markdown, sources, and citations — making it straightforward to parse and act on AI-generated responses rather than manually reading through free-form text.

### Geographic Targeting & Integrations

cloro supports global and state-level targeting for localized data extraction, and integrates with Python, JavaScript, n8n, Zapier, and LangChain, plus a hosted MCP server exposing its monitoring endpoints as agent tools.

## Cons

- ❌ Coverage and accuracy vary across different AI platforms as their own products evolve

---

## Who Is It Best For?

cloro is the right tool if you:

- **Want to know how AI assistants describe and cite your business** beyond traditional SEO tracking
- **Have a developer** who can build a dashboard or workflow on top of the raw API
- **Compete in a market where AI-driven research and recommendations matter**
- **Want one integration point** rather than separate connections to each AI platform

cloro is **not** the right tool if you:

- Need a polished, no-code dashboard without any developer involvement
- Are only focused on traditional Google rankings, not AI-generated answers
- Expect monitoring alone to improve citation rates without also improving content

## Final Verdict

cloro earns its place for businesses and developers who want direct, structured visibility into how AI search engines actually mention and cite them — a growing blind spot as more research shifts from Google rankings to AI-generated answers. Unifying multiple AI platforms behind one API removes the integration overhead of tracking each one separately.

The tool surfaces the problem clearly, but closing the gap — getting cited more often and more favorably — still comes down to the quality and structure of the content behind the brand, not the monitoring itself.

**Our recommendation:** Query a handful of questions a prospective client might actually ask an AI assistant about your business or services, and use what comes back to identify the biggest gaps in how you're currently being represented.
