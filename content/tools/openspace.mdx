## Overview

OpenSpace turns routine site walks into a continuously updated visual record. A worker mounts a 360° camera on a hard hat, walks the site, and OpenSpace automatically maps the captured imagery to floor plans and BIM within minutes — no manual tagging required.

On top of that capture, its AI tracks progress: comparing what's on site to the plan and schedule, flagging unfinished work or blockers, and generating reports. For general contractors managing multiple active sites, that turns documentation from a chore into usable jobsite intelligence.

This review evaluates OpenSpace on its capture speed, progress tracking, and fit for construction teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Fast 360° Reality Capture

Capture large areas in minutes with a helmet-mounted 360° camera; imagery is timestamped and automatically mapped to plans and BIM.

### AI Progress Tracking

OpenSpace compares captures against the schedule to track percent complete and planned-versus-actual progress, with alerts (Spotlights) for unfinished work or blockers.

### Objective Visual Record

A searchable, timestamped record of the site supports coordination, quality, and dispute resolution.

### Platform Integrations

It integrates with schedule and project tools including Procore and Autodesk Construction Cloud.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Fast 360° capture from a helmet-mounted camera, auto-mapped to plans and BIM
- ✅ AI progress tracking compares captures to the schedule and flags blockers
- ✅ Creates an objective visual record useful for coordination and disputes
- ✅ Integrates with platforms like Procore and Autodesk Construction Cloud

## Cons

- ❌ Most value comes on larger, BIM-driven projects with disciplined capture routines
- ❌ Requires consistent walking of the site to keep the record current
- ❌ Quote-based pricing tied to construction volume, not self-serve
- ❌ Progress insights complement rather than replace project management

---

## Who Is It Best For?

OpenSpace is the right tool if you:

- **Document progress** across one or more active sites
- **Want objective, timestamped records** for coordination and disputes
- **Run BIM-driven projects** tracking planned versus actual work
- **Manage multiple projects** and need consistent visibility

OpenSpace is **not** the right tool if you:

- Run small projects where a quick photo log is enough
- Can't maintain a disciplined capture routine
- Need full project management rather than capture and progress tracking

## Final Verdict

OpenSpace earns its place for general contractors who want reliable, objective progress data without dedicating staff to manual documentation. Fast 360° capture mapped to plans and BIM, plus AI progress tracking, makes routine site walks genuinely useful.

Its return is highest on larger, BIM-driven projects with a consistent capture routine, and pricing scales with construction volume.
