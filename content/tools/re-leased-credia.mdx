## Overview

Credia is the AI built into Re-Leased, a commercial property management platform. Rather than a standalone tool, it automates the operational paperwork that consumes property managers' time — across lease data, compliance documents, insurance records, invoices, and maintenance.

Credia has three parts: Credia Extract pulls key data from lease and other documents; Credia Advise answers natural-language questions across leases with citations to the source clause; and Credia Action reads emails and suggests tasks, reminders, and work orders. Crucially, it's designed to augment managers — every AI-suggested action requires human approval.

This review evaluates Re-Leased Credia on its automation, document intelligence, and fit for commercial property management in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Credia Extract

AI pulls key data points from leases and other documents — insurance, invoices, compliance certificates — cutting weeks of data entry to hours.

### Credia Advise

Natural-language Q&A across lease documents returns answers with citations to the exact source clause.

### Credia Action

Credia reads emails and suggests actions — maintenance tasks, reminders, notes, and work orders — within the workflow.

### Human-in-the-Loop

All AI-suggested actions require human approval before execution, keeping managers in control.

## Who Is It Best For?

Re-Leased Credia is the right tool if you:

- **Manage many commercial leases** and their paperwork
- **Are buried in compliance, invoice, and maintenance** documents
- **Want natural-language answers** from lease documents
- **Prefer AI suggestions with human oversight**

Re-Leased Credia is **not** the right tool if you:

- Work in residential agency rather than commercial property management
- Don't use (or want to adopt) the Re-Leased platform
- Want a standalone, self-serve document tool

## Final Verdict

Re-Leased Credia earns its place for commercial property managers who want AI embedded directly in their management platform, automating the lease, compliance, invoice, and maintenance paperwork that scales poorly by hand. The natural-language lease Q&A with citations and the human-approval model are both practical strengths.

It's tied to the Re-Leased platform and aimed at commercial property teams, so fit depends on that context.
