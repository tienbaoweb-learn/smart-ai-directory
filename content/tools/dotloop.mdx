## Overview

Dotloop is a real estate transaction management platform that moves deals off paper. It centralizes contracts, templates, e-signatures, task lists, and compliance review in a single workspace, so every step of a transaction lives in one place.

Its strength is keeping agents organized by client and transaction, with broad forms coverage through partnerships with 190+ local and state associations plus MLS feeds. For agents and brokerages that want deals to move smoothly from contract to close, it's a focused, well-established tool.

This review evaluates Dotloop on its transaction management, e-signatures, and fit for real estate teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Transaction Management

Dotloop organizes each deal as a "loop," centralizing documents, tasks, and status so nothing is duplicated or lost.

### E-Signatures and Documents

Built-in electronic signatures, multiparty signing, and document storage keep signing and paperwork in one system.

### Forms and Templates

Partnerships with 190+ associations plus MLS feeds provide comprehensive forms coverage, with task templates to speed setup.

### Compliance and Reporting

Compliance review, audit trails, and reporting tools give brokers visibility into performance and risk.

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Dotloop is the right tool if you:

- **Manage deals from contract to close**
- **Need compliance and audit trails** across transactions
- **Want unlimited transactions** with built-in e-signatures
- **Are replacing paper** and disconnected signing tools

Dotloop is **not** the right tool if you:

- Need a CRM or lead generation rather than transaction management
- Close too few deals to justify the subscription
- Want an all-in-one platform instead of a focused tool

## Final Verdict

Dotloop earns its place for agents, teams, and brokerages that want transactions organized, compliant, and paperless in one workspace. Broad forms coverage and unlimited transactions on Premium make it a dependable backbone for the deal side of the business.

It's deliberately focused on transactions rather than lead generation or CRM, so it pairs with those tools rather than replacing them.
