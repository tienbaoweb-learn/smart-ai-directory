## Overview

Claap is an AI-powered meeting and video workspace built around capturing, summarizing, and acting on conversations — automatically recording meetings across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, as well as screen recordings and uploads, then transcribing them in more than 99 languages and generating structured summaries that extract action items, decisions, and assigned responsibilities. Beyond live meetings, Claap is built for genuine asynchronous collaboration, aiming to reduce back-to-back meetings and endless message threads by letting teams record and review updates on their own time.

For architecture, construction, and real estate teams running frequent client and vendor calls, this matters because manual note-taking during a live meeting pulls attention away from the conversation itself — Claap's automatic transcription and structured summaries let someone actually focus on the discussion instead of typing notes.

This review evaluates Claap based on its recording and transcription accuracy, async collaboration features, and practical fit for meeting-heavy businesses heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Automatic Meeting Recording

Claap integrates directly with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, along with screen recordings and file uploads, capturing meetings whether they're live video calls or screen-share walkthroughs.

### AI Notes & Structured Summaries

Meetings are transcribed automatically in more than 99 languages, with AI-summary templates generating structured notes that extract action items, decisions, and assigned responsibilities rather than a flat transcript alone.

### Asynchronous Collaboration

Claap supports time-stamped comments and on-screen highlights, letting teams review and respond to a recorded update on their own schedule rather than requiring everyone present for a live meeting.

### Workflow Integrations

The platform connects with Notion, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and CRM systems, routing meeting notes and action items directly into the tools a team already uses day to day.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Automatically records meetings across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, plus screen shares
- ✅ Transcribes in 99+ languages and generates structured summaries with action items and decisions
- ✅ Built for async collaboration — time-stamped comments and highlights reduce redundant meetings
- ✅ Integrates with Notion, Slack, Teams, and CRM systems to route notes where the team already works

## Cons

- ❌ Value depends on a team actually running enough meetings to justify a dedicated recorder
- ❌ AI-extracted action items and decisions still benefit from a quick human review
- ❌ Async-first workflows require some team buy-in to replace default live-meeting habits
- ❌ Full automation across every integration takes initial setup and configuration

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Claap is the right tool if you:

- **Run frequent client, vendor, or internal meetings** that currently rely on manual note-taking
- **Want action items and decisions extracted automatically** rather than typed up after the fact
- **Are open to async video updates** replacing some recurring status meetings
- **Already use Notion, Slack, or a CRM** that meeting notes should flow into automatically

Claap is **not** the right tool if you:

- Run very few meetings where manual notes are already manageable
- Want a team fully committed to live meetings with no interest in async alternatives
- Need a tool focused purely on transcription without the collaboration and async layer

---

## Alternatives to Consider

- **Clipto** — more focused specifically on transcription and translation, without the meeting workspace layer
- **Wispr Flow** — relevant for dictating notes and follow-ups quickly rather than recording full meetings
- **Nifty** — relevant for tracking the action items Claap extracts inside a broader project workspace
- **monday.com** — better if you want AI agents acting across a full work management platform
- **Descript** — better if you also want to edit and repurpose meeting recordings into other content

---

## Final Verdict

Claap earns its place for meeting-heavy teams that want automatic recording and structured notes to replace manual note-taking, plus a genuine async layer to cut down on redundant live meetings. Extracting action items and decisions automatically — not just a raw transcript — is what separates it from a basic recorder.

The tool's biggest value shows up once a team commits to it across their regular meeting cadence — occasional use won't reveal the real time savings that come from consistent, automated documentation.

**Our recommendation:** Record your next few client meetings through Claap and compare the AI-generated action items against what you'd normally capture manually before rolling it out as your team's default meeting workflow.
