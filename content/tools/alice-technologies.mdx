## Overview

ALICE Technologies is an AI-powered construction optioneering platform that uses simulation to generate and compare construction schedule scenarios at a scale no human planner could achieve manually. Rather than producing a single baseline schedule, ALICE creates millions of possible execution sequences and evaluates them across variables like cost, duration, and resource allocation — surfacing the optimal options for project teams to review.

For construction firms working on complex, large-scale projects where scheduling inefficiency directly translates to budget overruns and timeline delays, ALICE addresses one of the most persistent planning challenges in the industry.

This review evaluates ALICE Technologies based on its planning capabilities, BIM integration, and practical fit for construction project teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### AI-Powered Schedule Optioneering

ALICE doesn't produce a single schedule — it generates millions of possible construction sequences and evaluates them across multiple variables simultaneously. Project teams can define their priorities (minimize duration, minimize cost, optimize labor) and ALICE returns ranked options rather than a single recommendation.

### BIM Integration

ALICE connects to building information models, pulling geometry and quantity data directly into its simulation engine. This means schedule scenarios are grounded in the actual project model rather than abstract assumptions, improving simulation accuracy.

### Rapid Re-Planning for Changes

When site conditions change — a subcontractor delays, materials arrive late, design modifications come in — ALICE can re-simulate the schedule based on updated inputs and surface new optimal paths, reducing the time needed to recover from disruptions.

### Resource and Cost Optimization

Beyond time, ALICE evaluates how different construction sequences affect labor crew requirements, equipment utilization, and total cost — enabling teams to make trade-off decisions with quantitative backing.

## Who Is It Best For?

ALICE Technologies is the right tool if you:

- **Manage large, complex construction projects** where schedule optimization has material cost impact
- **Already use BIM** and want to connect 3D models to schedule simulation
- **Need to evaluate multiple execution strategies** before committing to a baseline plan
- **Face frequent schedule disruptions** and need rapid re-planning capabilities

ALICE Technologies is **not** the right tool if you:

- Are managing small residential or fit-out projects where simpler scheduling tools suffice
- Don't have BIM models or structured project data to feed into the simulation
- Need a simple, low-cost project management tool rather than an enterprise planning platform

## Final Verdict

ALICE Technologies solves a genuinely hard problem in construction — how to evaluate the full space of possible construction sequences rather than defaulting to a single schedule built on planner intuition. For large commercial and infrastructure projects where optimizing time and cost has significant financial stakes, the simulation-based approach is a meaningful advance over traditional scheduling methods.

It's not the right tool for smaller projects or teams without existing BIM infrastructure, and the implementation investment is real. But for the right project scale, ALICE reduces the planning blindspot that leads to costly schedule assumptions.

**Our recommendation:** Evaluate ALICE on a real project during pre-construction planning, when you still have flexibility to adjust the execution sequence. Compare against your current baseline schedule — the gap between your planned approach and ALICE's optimized alternatives is the most direct evidence of its value.
