## Overview

Live demos don't scale. Every prospect who wants to see your product requires a scheduled call, a sales rep's time, and a calendar slot — and most prospects drop off before booking. Interactive demo tools solve this by letting prospects click through your product themselves, on their timeline, without friction.

Guideflow is built for this use case. It captures your product interface via a Chrome extension and transforms it into a guided, clickable walkthrough that sales teams can personalize per prospect, embed on websites, or share via link. No engineering required. No sandbox environment to manage.

The headline differentiator: bundled seat pricing that makes Guideflow significantly more affordable than per-user competitors at small team scale, with a $35/month Solo entry point that undercuts most tools in the category.

---

## Key Features

### No-Code Demo Capture

Install the Guideflow Chrome extension, walk through your product as you normally would, and Guideflow captures each screen automatically. The captured flow becomes a guided demo with navigation controls, tooltips, and step-by-step instructions.

For architecture firms, construction software vendors, or real estate platforms demoing their tools to prospects, this eliminates the need to maintain a separate demo environment or coordinate live sessions for every interested lead.

### Screenshot and HTML Capture Modes

Guideflow offers two capture methods:

**Screenshot mode** (available from $35/month): Captures static screenshots with interactive overlays. Clean and fast — ideal for straightforward product walkthroughs where pixel-perfect interactivity is not required.

**HTML capture** (Growth plan, $499/month): Captures the live DOM of your product, creating a fully interactive replica that prospects can click through as if using the real product. More immersive but significantly more expensive.

### Personalization

Demos can be personalized with the prospect's name, company, and relevant use case — making each walkthrough feel tailored rather than generic. Personalization variables are inserted via simple template fields, updated per share link without recreating the demo.

### Analytics and Engagement Tracking

Track how prospects engage with demos: which steps they complete, where they drop off, how long they spend on each screen, and whether they revisit specific sections. This engagement data surfaces intent signals for sales follow-up — a prospect who rewatches the pricing step three times is a different conversation than one who bounced at step two.

### CRM Integration

Demo engagement data integrates with CRM platforms, allowing sales teams to see prospect demo activity alongside other touchpoints in the deal timeline.

### Embedding and Sharing

Demos can be embedded on landing pages, shared via direct link, or sent in email sequences. For marketing teams running product-led growth strategies, the embed option allows website visitors to experience the product before signing up.

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | Seats | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1 | Limited demos, basic features |
| Solo | $35/month | 1 | Screenshot demos, analytics, sharing |
| Growth | $499/month | 10 | HTML capture, full interactivity, CRM integration |
| Advanced | $1,499/month | Custom | Enterprise features, SSO, advanced analytics |

The $35-to-$499 jump for HTML capture is the most significant limitation. Teams that need fully interactive demos face a pricing cliff that puts Guideflow Growth at the same price point as more feature-rich competitors like Storylane Growth.

**Best value tier:** Solo at $35/month for teams that need screenshot-based demos and value bundled seats over HTML interactivity.

## Who Is It Best For?

Guideflow is the right fit if you:

- Need interactive demos but cannot justify $400+/month for enterprise tools
- Run sales with screenshot-based walkthroughs rather than fully interactive product replicas
- Want bundled seat pricing to avoid per-user cost growth
- Are testing demo software and want a low-cost entry point

It is **not** the right fit if you:

- Need fully interactive HTML demos on a budget — the $499/month Growth requirement makes this expensive
- Require a large integration ecosystem with your existing sales stack
- Need advanced analytics comparable to enterprise demo platforms

## Alternatives to Consider

- **Storylane** — similar HTML capture capability; comparable Growth pricing; stronger analytics and integrations
- **Navattic** — enterprise-focused; stronger customization; higher pricing
- **Arcade** — strong for simple screenshot tours; good free tier; weaker on personalization
- **Reprise** — enterprise demo automation platform; most powerful but most expensive

## Final Verdict

Guideflow delivers on its core promise — no-code interactive demos with bundled seat pricing — at a price point that makes it accessible to small sales teams. The $35/month Solo plan is the lowest credible entry price in the category, and the Chrome extension capture is clean and fast.

The key limitation is the $35-to-$499 jump for HTML capture. Teams that need fully interactive demos should budget for Growth, and should evaluate whether Guideflow Growth delivers comparable value to Storylane or Navattic at similar price points.

**Our recommendation:** Start with the free plan. Build one demo of your core product flow and share it with 3-5 prospects. Measure engagement before committing to a paid tier.
