## Overview

Vertebrae was a pioneering 3D and augmented reality commerce platform that let furniture and home goods brands show true-to-scale products in customers' real spaces through web-based AR — no app download required. Its technology proved that AR visualization measurably lifted conversion and reduced returns for large, high-consideration purchases like furniture.

In 2021, Snap acquired Vertebrae, folding its team and technology into Snap's AR shopping ecosystem. As a result, Vertebrae no longer exists as a standalone platform that brands can purchase — its legacy continues within Snapchat's AR commerce capabilities, while the standalone 3D/AR commerce category it helped establish is now served by platforms like Threekit, Zakeke, and Cylindo.

This review covers what Vertebrae was, where its technology went, and what furniture brands seeking its capabilities should evaluate in 2026.

---

## Key Features (Historical)

### Web-Based AR Visualization

Vertebrae's signature capability was AR product viewing directly in the mobile browser — customers placed true-to-scale 3D furniture in their rooms from a product page link, removing the app-download friction that limited early AR adoption.

### 3D Product Asset Creation

The platform produced commerce-ready 3D models of products at scale, solving the content bottleneck that AR commerce depends on — a service dimension that remains central to today's AR platforms.

### Conversion-Focused Commerce Integration

Vertebrae embedded 3D and AR viewing into e-commerce product pages with analytics tying visualization engagement to conversion — building the measurement case that established AR's commercial value for furniture.

### Continuation in Snap AR

Post-acquisition, Vertebrae's technology and expertise strengthened Snap's AR shopping tools — where brands now reach shoppers with AR try-on and product placement experiences inside Snapchat's ecosystem.
