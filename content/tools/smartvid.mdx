## Overview

SmartVid — now known as Newmetrix — is a computer-vision platform that analyzes jobsite photos and videos to detect safety hazards, track compliance, and predict incidents. It's aimed squarely at construction safety, turning the imagery teams already capture into risk intelligence.

Its AI identifies hazards and PPE violations, scores projects against safety benchmarks, and predicts which projects are highest risk. Notably, it focuses on conditions rather than identifying individuals — there's no facial recognition in the system.

This review evaluates SmartVid/Newmetrix on its safety detection, risk analytics, and fit for construction firms in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Hazard and PPE Detection

Computer vision analyzes photos and videos to detect a wide range of safety hazards and PPE violations automatically.

### Safety Scoring and Prediction

The platform benchmarks projects with safety scores and predicts which projects pose the highest risk, helping teams prioritize.

### Field-System Integrations

It pulls imagery directly from tools like Procore and other field systems, so analysis runs on the photos teams already collect.

### Privacy-Conscious Design

The system focuses on conditions and hazards rather than identifying specific workers, with no facial recognition.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Computer vision detects 100+ safety hazards and PPE violations from photos and video
- ✅ Safety scoring benchmarks projects and predicts which are highest risk
- ✅ Pulls imagery from tools like Procore and other field systems automatically
- ✅ No facial recognition — focuses on conditions, not identifying workers

## Cons

- ❌ Value depends on a steady flow of jobsite photos and video
- ❌ Focused on safety analytics rather than broad project management
- ❌ Quote-based enterprise pricing, not self-serve
- ❌ AI flags require human review and corrective action to matter

---

## Who Is It Best For?

SmartVid is the right tool if you:

- **Lead safety** on higher-risk trades or large crews
- **Want objective safety benchmarking** across projects
- **Already capture lots of jobsite imagery** to analyze
- **Aim to reduce recordable incidents** with data

SmartVid is **not** the right tool if you:

- Don't capture enough jobsite photos or video for the AI to analyze
- Need broad project management rather than safety analytics
- Want a low-cost, self-serve tool

## Final Verdict

SmartVid/Newmetrix earns its place for construction firms that take safety seriously and already capture jobsite imagery. Turning that imagery into hazard detection, safety scores, and risk prediction offers a measurable, focused ROI through incident reduction.

It's a specialized safety tool rather than a broad platform, and it's priced for firms that will use it consistently.
