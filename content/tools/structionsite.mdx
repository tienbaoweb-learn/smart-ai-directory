## Overview

StructionSite, now part of DroneDeploy, is a construction documentation platform that captures site conditions through 360 video walkthroughs and photos pinned to floor plans — then applies AI to track installed work and verify progress. Field teams walk the site with a 360 camera, and the platform organizes the capture into a navigable, dated visual record of the entire project.

For general contractors and project managers, this systematic documentation serves multiple purposes: verifying subcontractor progress for payment applications, protecting against disputes with a dated visual record, and giving remote stakeholders visibility into site conditions without traveling.

This review evaluates StructionSite based on its capture workflow, AI progress tracking, and practical fit for construction project documentation in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### 360 Video Site Documentation

Field teams capture entire floors quickly by walking with a 360 camera — StructionSite maps the footage to floor plans automatically, producing a complete, navigable visual record without stopping to take individual photos.

### AI Progress Tracking

The platform's AI analyzes captured imagery to identify and quantify installed work — drywall, framing, MEP rough-in — supporting progress verification against schedule and more objective payment application reviews.

### Plan-Pinned Photo Organization

All captures are organized by location on the project's floor plans and by date, making it fast to find what a specific area looked like at any point in the project — a critical capability for dispute resolution and coordination.

### DroneDeploy Integration

As part of DroneDeploy, StructionSite connects interior documentation with aerial site capture, giving project teams a unified visual record spanning sitework through interior finishes.

---

## Who Is It Best For?

StructionSite is the right tool if you:

- **Run commercial construction projects** where documentation protects against disputes and claims
- **Verify progress for payment applications** and want objective visual evidence of installed work
- **Coordinate remote stakeholders** who need site visibility without site visits
- **Already use DroneDeploy** and want interior documentation in the same ecosystem

StructionSite is **not** the right tool if you:

- Build small residential projects where simple photo documentation suffices
- Can't establish a consistent capture routine, which the value depends on
- Need marketing-quality 3D tours rather than working documentation (consider Matterport)

## Alternatives to Consider

- **OpenSpace** — leading competitor with passive 360 capture and strong AI progress tracking
- **DroneDeploy** — the parent platform for aerial mapping and site documentation
- **Buildots** — AI construction progress tracking with deep schedule integration
- **HoloBuilder** — 360 construction documentation now part of Faro
- **Matterport** — higher-fidelity 3D capture better suited to marketing and as-built records

---

## Final Verdict

StructionSite delivers systematic, AI-enhanced construction documentation that pays for itself in dispute protection, progress verification, and remote visibility. The 360 walkthrough capture is fast enough to sustain as a regular site routine, and the plan-pinned organization makes years of site history genuinely searchable.

Its value depends on capture consistency — teams that walk the site on a regular cadence build a documentation asset; teams that capture sporadically get sporadic value. For commercial contractors, it's a proven documentation platform, best evaluated head-to-head against OpenSpace for workflow fit.

**Our recommendation:** Pilot StructionSite on one active project with a fixed weekly capture routine assigned to a specific team member — documentation tools succeed or fail on routine, and one project with disciplined capture will show you the real value before you roll it out across the portfolio.
