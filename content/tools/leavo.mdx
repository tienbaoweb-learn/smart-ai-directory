## Overview

Managing employee leave sounds simple until you are doing it at scale. A shared Google Sheet works for three people. It breaks down at ten. By twenty, you have scheduling conflicts, missed approvals, incorrect balance calculations, and compliance exposure — all from a tool never designed for the job.

Leavo is built specifically to replace that spreadsheet. It is a modern AI-powered HR leave management platform that handles time-off requests, multi-level approval workflows, leave balance tracking, holiday calendars, and labor law compliance in a single clean interface. And crucially, it is free for teams up to 9 members — with all features included.

For small and medium businesses in architecture, construction, real estate, and furniture — where team scheduling directly affects project delivery and client commitments — Leavo offers a practical, low-cost solution to an administrative problem that compounds as headcount grows.

---

## Key Features

### AI-Powered Leave Assistant

Leavo's AI assistant handles common leave management tasks conversationally. Team members can ask "How many days of annual leave do I have left?" or "Is the week of July 14 available for leave?" and receive instant, accurate answers without involving HR. The assistant also flags potential compliance issues — for example, when a leave request would violate minimum notice requirements under applicable labor law.

This reduces the volume of repetitive HR queries that consume time in small to mid-size teams.

### Leave Request and Approval Workflows

Employees submit time-off requests in seconds — specifying dates, leave type, and supporting notes. Managers receive instant notifications and approve or decline with a single action. Workflows support both single-step and multi-level approval hierarchies, with configurable escalation if an approver fails to respond within a defined timeframe.

Leave types are fully configurable: annual leave, sick leave, parental leave, unpaid leave, public holidays, and custom types defined by your organization.

### Real-Time Balance Tracking

Every team member has self-service access to current leave balances across all leave types. The system updates automatically the moment a request is approved, cancelled, or modified — no manual HR intervention required. Managers see a team-wide calendar view showing who is in and who is out at any given time, enabling better project and resource planning.

### Holiday Calendar Management

Configure public holiday calendars by country, region, or team — relevant for architecture and construction firms operating across multiple locations or jurisdictions. The calendar view integrates with team leave schedules to surface conflicts before they become problems.

### Labor Law Compliance

Leavo tracks leave accruals, statutory minimums, and carry-over rules in accordance with applicable labor law. The AI assistant flags requests that would violate policy or legal requirements before they are approved — reducing compliance risk for HR managers who may not have specialist legal knowledge.

This is particularly valuable for growing businesses where compliance exposure increases with headcount.

### Team Visibility Dashboard

A clean visual dashboard shows the entire team's leave calendar at a glance: upcoming absences, pending approvals, and leave patterns over time. Project managers in architecture and construction can plan resourcing around team availability without manually cross-referencing multiple spreadsheets or calendars.

## Use Cases by Industry

### Architecture & Construction

Project-based work makes leave management particularly important in these industries. A key architect or site manager being absent during a critical project phase can cascade into missed deadlines and client escalations. Leavo's team calendar and real-time availability view help project leads plan around leave before it becomes a resourcing crisis.

Construction firms with field and office staff can configure different leave policies for each team type — field workers may have different entitlements or approval workflows than administrative staff.

### Real Estate

Real estate agencies with sales teams and property managers need visibility into who is available to handle client inquiries and property showings. Leavo's team calendar makes coverage planning straightforward — managers can see at a glance whether sufficient staff will be available during peak periods (end of quarter, property expos, settlement periods).

### Furniture & Interior Design

Retail furniture businesses and interior design studios often have a mix of full-time staff, part-time employees, and contractors. Leavo's configurable leave types and approval workflows accommodate this diversity without requiring separate systems for different employment categories.

## Who Is It Best For?

Leavo is the right fit if you:

- Are managing leave on spreadsheets or email and want a purpose-built tool
- Have a team of 2–9 people and want a free, full-featured solution
- Need labor law compliance without the cost and complexity of enterprise HRIS
- Want a clean, modern interface your team will actually use without training

It is **not** the right fit if you:

- Need a full HRIS (payroll, recruiting, performance management) — Leavo is leave-focused
- Require deep integrations with existing payroll or ERP systems
- Are a large enterprise with complex multi-jurisdiction compliance requirements
- Need advanced workforce analytics and reporting
