## Overview

Mastt is a capital project management platform focused on cost, contracts, payments, and reporting. Built for project owners, owner's representatives, and project managers, it centralizes the financial controls that otherwise sprawl across spreadsheets.

Its AI layer scans contracts, payments, and invoices, runs approval checks, and pre-fills the cost module — reducing manual data entry and keeping dashboards and forecasts current. The result is audit-ready records and faster reporting to stakeholders.

This review evaluates Mastt on its cost controls, AI document handling, and fit for capital projects in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Cost and Contract Controls

Mastt tracks budgets, forecasts, actuals, commitments, variance, contracts, payments, and approvals across projects and programs.

### AI Document Processing

AI scans contracts, payments, and invoices, performs approval checks, and pre-fills forms into the cost module to cut manual entry.

### Live Dashboards and Reporting

Real-time dashboards and standardized reporting give clients and stakeholders a current view without spreadsheet rebuilds.

### Portfolio and Program View

Built for capital projects and portfolios, it supports program-level oversight with audit-ready records.

## Who Is It Best For?

Mastt is the right tool if you:

- **Own or represent** capital projects and control the spend
- **Track budgets, forecasts, and variance** across projects
- **Manage contracts, payments, and approvals** at scale
- **Need audit-ready records** and fast client reporting

Mastt is **not** the right tool if you:

- Need site-execution tools rather than cost and contract controls
- Run small, one-off jobs where spreadsheets suffice
- Want field capture or scheduling as your primary need

## Final Verdict

Mastt earns its place for owners and project managers who need tight cost and contract control on capital projects without living in spreadsheets. The AI document handling — scanning contracts, payments, and invoices and pre-filling the cost module — targets a real, repetitive bottleneck in project controls.

It's built for the owner and controls side rather than site execution, and its full AI value sits on the paid tiers.
