## Overview

Buildots is a construction technology platform that automates site progress tracking by capturing 360° photos as workers walk the site with a helmet-mounted camera, then using computer vision to compare what's actually built against the BIM model and project schedule. Instead of a superintendent manually documenting progress room by room, the system flags discrepancies and tracks completion automatically.

For general contractors managing multiple active sites, this removes one of the most time-consuming and error-prone parts of project management: knowing, with confidence, exactly how much of the work is actually done versus what the schedule says should be done.

This review evaluates Buildots based on its capture workflow, BIM comparison accuracy, and practical fit for active commercial construction projects heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Automated 360° Site Capture

Workers wear a helmet-mounted 360° camera during routine site walks, automatically capturing visual documentation of every space without requiring a dedicated person to stop and photograph each area manually.

### BIM-Based Progress Comparison

Captured imagery is compared directly against the BIM model using computer vision, automatically detecting what's been completed, what's missing, and where the as-built condition deviates from the design.

### Schedule & Trade Tracking

Beyond raw progress, Buildots ties detected work back to the project schedule and specific trades, helping pinpoint exactly where delays are occurring rather than just flagging that the project overall is behind.

### Searchable Visual History

Every capture builds a searchable, time-stamped visual record of the site, letting teams look back at exactly what a space looked like on a specific date — useful for resolving disputes or verifying claims after the fact.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Automated 360° capture removes manual walkthrough documentation
- ✅ Direct comparison against the BIM model flags deviations automatically
- ✅ Reduces time spent reconciling actual progress against the schedule
- ✅ Photo-based record creates a clear, searchable history of every space over time

## Cons

- ❌ Requires consistent site capture routines to get full value
- ❌ Most valuable on larger, BIM-driven projects rather than small renovations
- ❌ Setup and onboarding takes real coordination with the project team
- ❌ Pricing is typically quoted per project, requiring a sales conversation

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Buildots is the right tool if you:

- **Manage large, BIM-modeled commercial projects** where manual progress tracking is impractical
- **Need objective, photo-based evidence** of progress rather than relying on verbal updates
- **Want to catch deviations from design intent early**, before they become costly rework
- **Run multiple active sites** and need a consistent, scalable way to monitor all of them

Buildots is **not** the right tool if you:

- Work on small renovation or residential projects without a detailed BIM model
- Don't have the team buy-in needed to maintain consistent site capture routines
- Need a lightweight tool without the setup involved in a full BIM-integrated platform

---

## Alternatives to Consider

Buildots is strong for automated, BIM-based progress tracking, but it is not the only option:

- **Procore AI** — broader construction management platform with AI agents across documents and workflows
- **PlanGrid** — simpler document and field management, now in maintenance mode under Autodesk
- **TestFit** — focused on early-stage feasibility and site planning rather than active progress tracking
- **Insightful** — better if your core need is office productivity tracking rather than site progress
- **Team Pulse** — lighter-weight project visibility without computer-vision site capture

---

## Final Verdict

Buildots earns its place for general contractors who need reliable, objective progress data across active commercial sites without dedicating staff time to manual documentation. Tying captured imagery directly to the BIM model turns routine site walks into a continuously updated progress record.

The system delivers the most value on larger, BIM-driven projects with disciplined capture routines — smaller or less BIM-mature projects may not see the same return.

**Our recommendation:** Pilot Buildots on a single active project with a strong BIM model before rolling it out across your full portfolio, so your team can build the capture routine before scaling up.
