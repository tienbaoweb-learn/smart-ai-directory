## Overview

Most CRM platforms are built for inside sales teams at desks with large screens. Leader takes a different approach: it is a mobile-first CRM designed for the reality of how many SMB sales and relationship management actually happens — in the field, on-site, and on the move.

For architecture firms visiting client sites, real estate agents showing properties, construction managers coordinating with clients, or furniture showroom staff following up with leads, the ability to update CRM records, track pipeline, and manage contacts from a mobile device is not a nice-to-have — it is the difference between a CRM that gets used and one that does not.

Leader centralizes lead management, contact tracking, and sales pipeline in a clean mobile interface, available in 20+ languages for international teams.

## Key Features

### Mobile-First CRM

Leader is designed from the ground up for mobile use — not a desktop CRM with a mobile app bolted on. The interface prioritizes the tasks field teams do most: logging a call after a client meeting, updating a lead status on-site, checking pipeline before a sales conversation, and adding contact notes immediately after an interaction.

This mobile-first approach reduces the friction that causes CRM adoption to fail in field-based teams.

### Lead Management

Capture, track, and manage leads through a configurable pipeline. Assign leads to team members, set follow-up reminders, track lead source, and monitor conversion rates from first contact to closed deal.

For architecture and construction firms where a single project lead can represent significant revenue, structured lead tracking with follow-up accountability is a meaningful operational improvement over spreadsheets or email.

### Contact and Client Database

Maintain a centralized contact database with full interaction history: calls, meetings, emails, notes, and documents associated with each contact. For real estate agencies managing multiple agents across a territory, centralized contact data prevents duplicate outreach and ensures continuity when team members change.

### Pipeline Tracking

Visual pipeline views show deal stages, values, and expected close dates across the sales team. Managers get visibility into team performance and pipeline health without chasing individual updates.

### Multilingual Support

Available in 20+ languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and more. Relevant for internationally operating architecture firms, construction companies with multilingual teams, or furniture businesses serving diverse client bases.

## Use Cases by Industry

### Architecture & Construction

Project-based businesses run on relationships and lead tracking. From the first inquiry through proposal, contract signing, project delivery, and referral, every client interaction should be logged and followed up. Leader's mobile-first approach means project managers and business development staff can update records after site visits and client meetings without returning to the office.

### Real Estate

Real estate agencies with distributed agent teams benefit from centralized lead tracking and contact management. When a lead comes in from any source — website, referral, property portal — it enters the pipeline and gets assigned to the right agent with follow-up accountability. Leader's mobile interface matches how agents work.

### Furniture & Interior Design

Furniture showrooms and interior design studios manage a sales cycle that involves multiple touchpoints: initial showroom visit, quote, sample approval, order, delivery, and follow-up. CRM tracking ensures no client falls through the cracks between touchpoints, and that the full relationship history is available to any team member who interacts with the client.
