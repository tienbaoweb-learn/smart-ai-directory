## Overview

GetIllustrations is a library of over 170,000 hand-drawn vector illustrations and icons, spanning more than 400 illustration packs in styles ranging from flat and outline to hand-drawn, 3D, and isometric. Rather than commissioning a designer for every illustration a website or app needs, businesses can license existing illustration packs or order custom illustrations matching an existing style — or an entirely new one built for their brand.

For architecture, construction, furniture, and real estate businesses building out a website, app, or marketing materials, having access to a large, consistent illustration library removes the cost and lead time of commissioning original artwork for every visual need.

This review evaluates GetIllustrations based on its library breadth, licensing terms, and practical fit for businesses needing polished visual assets heading into 2026.

## Key Features

### Large, Growing Illustration Library

With over 170,000 illustrations and icons across 400+ packs, the library covers a wide range of subjects and styles, with new additions made regularly rather than a static, aging collection.

### Multiple Illustration Styles

Styles span flat, outline, hand-drawn, 3D, and isometric vectors, letting businesses choose a visual direction that matches their brand rather than being limited to one aesthetic.

### Custom Illustration Service

Beyond the existing library, GetIllustrations offers a custom design service for businesses wanting a unique mascot or a tailored illustration system built specifically for their brand, with discounts available for active subscribers.

### Design Tool Compatibility

Illustrations are provided in formats compatible with Figma, Sketch, and Adobe tools, alongside code-friendly SVG and PNG formats, fitting into existing design and development workflows without conversion hassle.

## Who Is It Best For?

GetIllustrations is the right tool if you:

- **Need polished illustrations for a website or app** without commissioning original art for each
- **Want design-tool-compatible assets** that fit directly into Figma, Sketch, or code
- **Are building a consistent visual style** across multiple pages or marketing materials
- **Might eventually want a custom mascot or illustration system** unique to your brand

GetIllustrations is **not** the right tool if you:

- Need instant, AI-generated custom illustrations rather than human-made artwork
- Only need a one-time illustration and don't want an ongoing licensing relationship
- Require pixel-perfect brand-matched illustrations without any curation effort

## Final Verdict

GetIllustrations earns its place as a practical source of polished, licensed visual assets for businesses that need illustrations regularly but don't want to commission custom artwork every time. The breadth of styles and the option to order custom work when needed covers both quick, generic needs and more brand-specific requirements.

Because the illustrations are human-made rather than AI-generated, custom requests take real production time — plan ahead rather than expecting instant turnaround for a unique brand illustration system.

**Our recommendation:** Start with a free or lower-cost pack that matches your brand's visual style before committing to an All-Access plan, and consider the custom service only once you know exactly what unique illustration your brand needs.
