## Overview

Help.center is an AI-powered knowledge base platform that gives businesses a hosted self-service website — at yourcompany.help.center or a custom domain — along with an AI chat widget that can answer customer questions conversationally based on the published help content. Rather than customers searching through articles manually, the AI search and chat features try to surface or generate the right answer directly.

For furniture retailers, real estate agencies, and contractors who field a steady stream of repetitive questions — shipping policies, listing details, service processes — this matters because every question a customer can resolve themselves is one less ticket, email, or phone call a support team has to handle manually.

This review evaluates Help.center based on its setup speed, AI answer quality, and practical fit for small support teams heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Hosted Help Center Website

Businesses get a ready-to-use help center website at a yourcompany.help.center subdomain, or their own custom domain, without needing to build and host a knowledge base site from scratch.

### AI Search & Chat Widget

Customers can use AI-powered search on the help center site, and businesses can deploy an AI chat widget on their main website to answer questions conversationally, grounded in the published help content.

### One-Click Knowledge Base Import

Existing help articles from platforms like Intercom, Crisp, Zendesk, and Freshdesk can be imported in one click, making migration practical rather than requiring manual re-entry of every article.

### Shared Email Inbox

Beyond self-service, Help.center includes a shared email inbox for managing and replying to customer conversations, consolidating support communication channels in one place.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Hosted help center website is live quickly at yourcompany.help.center
- ✅ AI search and chat widget give customers instant answers without a ticket
- ✅ One-click import from existing knowledge bases like Zendesk and Intercom
- ✅ Custom domain support lets the help center match your own branding

## Cons

- ❌ AI answer quality depends entirely on how complete your knowledge base content is
- ❌ Migrating and refining imported articles still takes some manual review
- ❌ Best value comes once you have a real volume of repetitive questions to deflect
- ❌ Shared inbox features add value mainly for teams handling email-based support too

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Help.center is the right tool if you:

- **Field repetitive customer questions** that a well-written help article could answer
- **Want AI to surface answers conversationally** rather than making customers search manually
- **Already have help content elsewhere** that can be imported rather than rewritten from scratch
- **Want to reduce ticket volume** without adding support headcount

Help.center is **not** the right tool if you:

- Don't yet have enough help content for the AI to ground accurate answers in
- Have very low support volume where a simple FAQ page would suffice
- Need deep, complex ticketing workflows beyond a shared inbox and knowledge base

---

## Alternatives to Consider

Help.center is strong for AI-powered self-service support, but it is not the only option:

- **Collect.chat** — better for lead capture and appointment booking rather than help content
- **CustomGPT.ai** — comparable grounded Q&A chatbot with deeper document training options
- **Algomo** — broader AI chatbot and helpdesk platform across multiple channels
- **Gallabox** — better if your primary support channel is WhatsApp rather than a help center
- **Setmore** — relevant if scheduling, not support deflection, is your main need

---

## Final Verdict

Help.center earns its place for small businesses that get enough repetitive questions to justify a proper self-service knowledge base, but don't have the resources to build and host one separately. The combination of a hosted help site, AI chat, and easy migration from existing platforms removes most of the setup friction.

The tool is only as good as the content behind it — investing time in writing clear, complete help articles will determine how useful the AI search and chat actually are.

**Our recommendation:** Import or write your 10 most frequently asked questions first, and measure ticket deflection before expanding your knowledge base further.
