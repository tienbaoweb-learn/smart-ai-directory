## Overview

Handoff is AI-native software built specifically for residential contractors, home builders, and remodelers, covering the full journey from lead to signed contract to completed project. Rather than treating estimating, CRM, and project management as separate tools, Handoff lets users upload construction drawings, scopes of work, client notes, photos, and videos, then uses AI to analyze that material and generate detailed estimates, invoices, and project scopes.

What sets it apart is that the AI learns from a contractor's own past projects and pricing rules over time, becoming more accurate the more it's used — rather than applying generic industry averages that don't reflect a specific business's actual cost structure and markup approach.

This review evaluates Handoff based on its estimating accuracy, pipeline management depth, and practical fit for residential contracting businesses heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### AI-Powered Estimating from Raw Project Material

Handoff can analyze uploaded drawings, scopes of work, client notes, photos, and videos to generate detailed estimates, removing much of the manual data entry that traditionally goes into building a quote.

### Learning From Past Projects

The AI becomes smarter with use, learning from a contractor's historical projects and pricing rules so that estimates reflect that specific business's actual costs and markup structure rather than generic averages.

### Custom Pricing & Markup Rules

Handoff supports multi-level markups, separate labor and material rates, and room-by-room cost breakdowns, giving contractors the granularity needed for accurate, detailed estimates on complex remodels.

### Full Lead-to-Completion Pipeline

Beyond estimating, Handoff covers CRM and pipeline management to turn leads into signed contracts, plus project management from estimate through completion, including scheduling, task tracking, and job documentation.

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Handoff is the right tool if you:

- **Run a residential contracting, home building, or remodeling business** generating frequent estimates
- **Want estimates built from real project material** — drawings, photos, notes — rather than manual entry
- **Need detailed, room-by-room pricing breakdowns** for complex remodel projects
- **Want CRM, estimating, and project management** in one connected platform

Handoff is **not** the right tool if you:

- Work primarily in commercial or industrial construction rather than residential
- Are just starting out with little project history for the AI to learn from yet
- Need a narrow, single-purpose estimating tool rather than a full business platform

---

## Alternatives to Consider

Handoff is strong for residential contractor estimating and pipeline management, but it is not the only option:

- **Procore AI** — better suited for larger commercial construction management with AI agents
- **Buildots** — focused on automated progress tracking rather than estimating and CRM
- **TestFit** — relevant earlier in the process for site feasibility rather than estimating
- **PlanGrid** — narrower focus on document and field management, now in maintenance mode
- **LeadTruffle** — relevant for capturing and qualifying inbound leads rather than estimating

---

## Final Verdict

Handoff earns its place for residential contractors who want AI to do the heavy lifting on estimating — turning drawings, photos, and notes into a detailed quote — while also managing the client relationship from first contact through project completion. The fact that it learns from a contractor's actual pricing history, rather than generic industry data, is a meaningful differentiator for accuracy over time.

The platform's value compounds with use: more project history means more accurate future estimates, so the experience improves the longer a business sticks with it.

**Our recommendation:** Upload a handful of recently completed projects first to give the AI a real pricing baseline before relying on it heavily for new estimates.
