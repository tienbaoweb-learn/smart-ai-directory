## Overview

Kreo is an AI-powered construction estimating and quantity takeoff platform that automates the manual measurement process traditionally performed by estimators reviewing 2D drawings and 3D BIM models. By using AI to identify, classify, and measure building elements automatically, Kreo reduces the time required to produce quantity takeoffs — one of the most labor-intensive and error-prone parts of the preconstruction process.

The platform targets contractors and estimating teams who face pressure to turn around accurate bids faster, with less manual effort, across an increasing volume of projects. Kreo positions itself as a complement to estimators' expertise, handling the measurement burden so estimators can focus on pricing strategy and scope interpretation.

This review evaluates Kreo based on its takeoff accuracy, workflow integration, and practical fit for construction estimating teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### AI Quantity Takeoff from 2D Drawings

Kreo's core capability automatically identifies and measures building elements from 2D PDF construction drawings — walls, slabs, openings, finishes, and other elements — producing quantity lists that would otherwise require hours of manual measurement.

### BIM Model Integration

For projects with 3D BIM models, Kreo can extract quantities directly from model geometry, leveraging the information already embedded in BIM files to produce faster, more comprehensive takeoffs without redoing measurement from 2D documents.

### Cloud-Based Collaborative Estimating

The platform runs in the cloud, allowing estimating teams to work on the same project simultaneously, share takeoffs internally, and collaborate with subcontractors or owners without the file version management issues of desktop software.

### Cost Database Integration

Kreo connects with standard construction cost databases and allows teams to apply pricing to extracted quantities, creating a path from takeoff to cost estimate within the same platform.

## Who Is It Best For?

Kreo is the right tool if you:

- **Handle a high volume of bids** where takeoff time is the primary constraint on how many projects you can pursue
- **Want to reduce manual measurement errors** that affect estimate accuracy and project profitability
- **Manage an estimating team** that would benefit from collaborative, cloud-based workflow rather than desktop-only tools
- **Work with BIM models** and want to extract quantities from 3D model data rather than 2D drawings alone

Kreo is **not** the right tool if you:

- Are a small contractor with low bid volume where manual takeoff time isn't a significant bottleneck
- Need a lightweight, low-cost takeoff solution without enterprise pricing
- Work primarily with handwritten or scanned paper drawings that don't lend themselves to AI recognition

## Final Verdict

Kreo addresses a genuine efficiency problem in construction preconstruction — manual quantity takeoff is time-consuming, error-prone, and a bottleneck on bid capacity for estimating teams. The AI automation it brings to this specific task has clear productivity value for firms dealing with bid volume pressure.

The platform is best suited to mid-to-large contractors and estimating teams where the time savings justify the enterprise investment. For smaller firms or independent estimators, the cost-benefit calculation may favor lighter alternatives.

**Our recommendation:** Pilot Kreo on a set of similar past projects where you already know the accurate quantities — comparing AI takeoff output against your actual verified quantities is the most reliable way to calibrate confidence in the tool before using it for live bid production.
