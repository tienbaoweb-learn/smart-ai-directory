## Overview

Lofty (formerly Chime) is an all-in-one real estate platform that combines a CRM, an IDX website, lead generation, and marketing automation in a single system. Rather than running a separate CRM, website, and ad tool, agents and teams manage the whole pipeline — from lead capture to follow-up — in one login.

The platform targets agents and teams who want a standardised, end-to-end system, and it's frequently offered at a discount or bundled through brokerages, which materially changes the cost calculation for individual agents. Its all-in-one breadth is its main draw and, as the trade-off, the source of its main criticism.

This review evaluates Lofty based on its CRM, lead generation, marketing automation, and practical fit for real estate agents and teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### All-in-One CRM

Lofty's CRM sits at the center of the platform, tracking leads through the pipeline and keeping contact history, tasks, and follow-up in one place rather than in a separate system.

### IDX Website and Lead Capture

Lofty includes an IDX website that surfaces listings and captures leads directly into the CRM, so the site and the database are part of the same system.

### Built-In Lead Generation

The platform bundles many lead-generation methods aimed at keeping an agent or team's pipeline full, reducing reliance on separate advertising tools.

### AI Follow-Up and Marketing Automation

AI-driven follow-up and marketing automation handle routine nurture — reminders, campaigns, and pipeline actions — so leads don't go cold while agents focus on active clients.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ All-in-one platform: CRM, IDX website, lead capture, and marketing in one login
- ✅ Many built-in lead-generation methods aimed at keeping an agent or team's pipeline full
- ✅ AI-driven follow-up and automation reduce manual pipeline management
- ✅ Often available at a discount or bundled through brokerages, which changes the value math

## Cons

- ❌ Customer support is frequently cited as inconsistent in independent user reviews
- ❌ Breadth means a setup that rewards tech-savvy agents; it's powerful but not fully plug-and-play
- ❌ All-in-one lock-in makes it harder to swap out a single component you dislike
- ❌ Quote-based pricing can be a barrier for solo agents without a brokerage subsidy

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Lofty is the right tool if you:

- **Want one system** for CRM, website, lead gen, and marketing instead of separate tools
- **Have a brokerage** that subsidises or bundles the platform
- **Value built-in lead generation** alongside an IDX website
- **Are willing to invest setup time** to standardise your whole workflow

Lofty is **not** the right tool if you:

- Already use a CRM you like and only want a dedicated marketing or lead-gen layer on top
- Need highly responsive, hands-on support as a baseline requirement
- Are a solo agent who can't justify quote-based, all-in-one pricing without a subsidy

## Final Verdict

Lofty earns its place for agents and teams who want a single system to run the whole business — CRM, IDX website, lead generation, and marketing — rather than integrating separate tools. Its strongest case is for teams that value that standardisation, and especially for agents whose brokerage subsidises the cost.

The recurring criticism is support: independent reviews frequently flag it as inconsistent, and the platform rewards agents willing to invest in setup. Those are real trade-offs for the breadth it offers.

**Our recommendation:** If your brokerage offers Lofty at a discount, it's worth evaluating as your single system — but confirm current pricing and test the onboarding before committing. If you already have a CRM you like, compare it against a dedicated marketing layer like [Ylopo](/tools/ylopo) before switching everything over.
