## Overview

BoomTown is an established real estate lead generation and CRM platform built for teams and brokerages — combining managed digital advertising, an IDX consumer website, and a team-oriented CRM with the accountability tooling that structured follow-up operations require. Now part of Inside Real Estate (which also owns kvCORE), BoomTown has long been a fixture among high-producing teams that treat lead conversion as a managed process.

The platform's model is full-service: BoomTown runs the advertising that fills the pipeline, its website captures and tracks lead behavior, and the CRM structures follow-up with task plans, routing, and dashboards that let team leaders manage conversion as an operation rather than an individual effort.

This review evaluates BoomTown based on its lead generation, CRM depth, and practical fit for real estate teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Managed Lead Generation

BoomTown manages digital advertising campaigns on behalf of teams — generating buyer and seller leads at consistent volume without requiring in-house ad expertise.

### Team CRM with Accountability

The CRM is built around team operations: lead routing, follow-up task plans, and accountability dashboards that show leaders exactly who is working their pipeline and where conversion is breaking down.

### IDX Website with Intent Tracking

The consumer search website captures leads and tracks their behavior — search patterns, favorites, and return visits — surfacing intent signals that help agents prioritize follow-up on the leads most likely to transact.

### Predictive Insights

BoomTown applies predictive analysis to lead behavior, flagging when past leads show renewed activity that suggests they're re-entering the market — turning the database into an ongoing opportunity source.

---

## Who Is It Best For?

BoomTown is the right tool if you:

- **Run a team** with multiple agents working a shared lead pipeline
- **Want managed lead generation** rather than running advertising yourself
- **Manage by metrics** and need accountability dashboards for follow-up discipline
- **Have the budget and volume** where a full-service platform pays for itself

BoomTown is **not** the right tool if you:

- Are a solo agent early in your business where the investment outpaces your pipeline
- Already have a CRM you like and only want a lead generation layer (consider Ylopo)
- Want a lightweight tool without operational onboarding commitment

## Alternatives to Consider

- **Lofty** — all-in-one platform with strong AI follow-up automation
- **kvCORE** — sister platform under Inside Real Estate with broad brokerage adoption
- **Ylopo** — dedicated lead generation layer that pairs with your existing CRM
- **Follow Up Boss** — focused team CRM paired with separate lead sources
- **Sierra Interactive** — strong IDX and lead generation with CRM included

---

## Final Verdict

BoomTown remains a proven platform for teams that treat lead conversion as a structured operation — its managed lead generation plus accountability-focused CRM matches how high-producing teams actually run. The full-service model works best for established operations with the budget and discipline to work a consistent pipeline.

With BoomTown now under Inside Real Estate alongside kvCORE, prospective buyers should ask about platform direction and long-term roadmap during evaluation. For teams comparing options, the practical decision usually comes down to BoomTown/kvCORE's all-in-one model versus pairing a focused CRM like Follow Up Boss with a dedicated lead layer like Ylopo.

**Our recommendation:** Before signing, ask BoomTown for market-specific cost-per-lead and conversion benchmarks for teams your size in your metro — lead economics vary widely by market, and validated local numbers matter more than platform features in deciding whether managed lead generation will pay off for your operation.
