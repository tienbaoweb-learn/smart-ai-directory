## Overview

Termly is a legal compliance platform that generates privacy policies, terms of service, cookie consent banners, and other compliance documentation for websites — using a structured, AI-assisted generation process rather than requiring custom legal drafting for every business. For real estate, furniture, and design businesses running websites that collect customer or visitor data, Termly addresses the practical compliance requirements that come with operating online in an era of GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.

Beyond document generation, Termly includes a cookie consent management system that scans a website's cookies and presents compliant consent options to visitors, which is increasingly required by privacy law in multiple jurisdictions.

This review evaluates Termly based on its document generation quality, compliance coverage, and practical fit for real estate and design industry websites in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Privacy Policy and Terms Generator

Termly's generator walks businesses through a structured questionnaire about their data collection practices and website functionality, producing a privacy policy and terms of service tailored to those specifics rather than a generic template that doesn't match actual practices.

### Cookie Consent Management

The platform scans a website to identify cookies and tracking technologies in use, then deploys a compliant consent banner that allows visitors to manage their preferences — addressing requirements under GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, and similar regulations.

### Multi-Regulation Compliance Coverage

Termly's documentation covers major privacy regulations including GDPR (EU), CCPA/CPRA (California), and other regional requirements, which is particularly relevant for real estate and design businesses serving clients across multiple jurisdictions.

### Automatic Policy Updates

As underlying privacy regulations change, Termly updates its generated policy templates, reducing the burden of manually tracking legal changes and updating website documentation accordingly.

## Who Is It Best For?

Termly is the right tool if you:

- **Run a website that collects visitor or lead data** through contact forms, chat, or e-commerce
- **Need GDPR or CCPA-compliant cookie consent** but don't have in-house legal or compliance staff
- **Want compliance documentation that updates automatically** as regulations change
- **Are a small-to-mid-size business** without budget for ongoing custom legal drafting

Termly is **not** the right tool if you:

- Have complex, jurisdiction-specific legal requirements that need bespoke legal counsel
- Operate in a heavily regulated industry where generic compliance templates aren't sufficient
- Already have legal counsel actively managing your privacy and compliance documentation

## Final Verdict

Termly addresses a real and often overlooked operational requirement for real estate, furniture, and design businesses running websites that collect customer data — legally structured privacy documentation and cookie consent that most small businesses don't have in-house legal resources to produce themselves.

The generated documents are not a substitute for legal advice in complex situations, but for standard website compliance needs, Termly's combination of policy generation, cookie consent management, and automatic regulation updates covers the practical requirements most design and real estate businesses face.
