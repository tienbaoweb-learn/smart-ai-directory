## Overview

V7 Go, from V7 Labs, is an AI agent platform for document intelligence, and one of its strongest use cases is commercial real estate. It automates the document-heavy work that slows CRE teams — most notably lease abstraction — by reading documents and extracting the terms that matter.

Its lease abstraction agent reads a lease and pulls 100+ key terms, dates, and clauses in minutes, with every extracted data point backed by a visual citation so a reviewer can click to the exact source. Beyond leases, specialized agents cover market analysis, risk evaluation, and valuation.

This review evaluates V7 Go on its document automation, accuracy, and fit for commercial real estate teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### AI Lease Abstraction

V7 Go automatically extracts 100+ key terms, dates, and clauses from commercial leases in minutes, compressing weeks of manual work.

### Visual Citations

Every abstracted data point links to the exact source clause, so teams can verify any term instantly.

### Specialized CRE Agents

Agents cover lease analysis, market research, risk evaluation, and property valuation for document-driven workflows.

### High Accuracy on Complex Documents

The platform reports strong accuracy rates across complex, unstructured documents.

## Who Is It Best For?

V7 Go is the right tool if you:

- **Abstract leases at volume** in commercial real estate
- **Extract terms, dates, and clauses** across a portfolio
- **Run market, risk, or valuation analysis** on documents
- **Want to replace weeks** of manual document review

V7 Go is **not** the right tool if you:

- Need an agent CRM or lead-gen tool rather than document AI
- Process too few documents to justify a usage-based platform
- Want a simple, flat published price

## Final Verdict

V7 Go earns its place for commercial real estate teams drowning in document work. AI lease abstraction with visual citations turns a slow, error-prone manual process into minutes of reviewable output, and the specialized agents extend that into analysis.

It's a document-intelligence platform for CRE workflows rather than an agent CRM, and it's priced by usage.

**Our recommendation:** Pilot V7 Go on a batch of real leases and check both the extracted terms and the visual citations against a manual abstraction for accuracy and time saved.
