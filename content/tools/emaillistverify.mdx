## Key Features

### 8-Check Verification Process

Every email address is run through eight distinct verification checks:

**1. Syntax validation** — checks email format against RFC standards (catches typos, missing @, invalid characters)

**2. Domain and MX record lookup** — verifies the domain exists and has valid mail exchange records

**3. SMTP validation** — connects to the mail server to verify the specific mailbox exists without sending an email

**4. Spam trap detection** — identifies honeypot addresses maintained by ISPs and anti-spam organizations to catch bulk senders

**5. Catch-all domain detection** — flags domains configured to accept all emails regardless of whether the specific mailbox exists

**6. Disposable email filtering** — identifies temporary email addresses from services like Mailinator, Guerrilla Mail, and similar

**7. Hard bounce detection** — removes addresses that have previously generated hard bounces

**8. Duplicate removal** — deduplicates your list automatically during processing

### Bulk List Verification

Upload lists in TXT, CSV, or XLSX format. Processing speed supports hundreds of thousands of addresses per hour — relevant for marketing teams cleaning large databases before major campaign sends. Results are returned with granular status classifications (Valid, Invalid, Unknown, Catch-all, Disposable, Spam Trap) for precise segmentation decisions.

### Real-Time Verification API

Integrate email verification directly into signup forms, CRM data entry, or lead capture pages. Invalid emails are caught at the point of entry rather than discovered after the fact in bounce reports. API documentation covers JavaScript, Python, PHP, and other common languages.

## Accuracy: Honest Assessment

EmailListVerify claims 99% deliverability assurance. Independent benchmarks place accuracy at 97-98% — lower than premium tools like ZeroBounce (99.3%) or Bouncer (99.2%).

What this means in practice: for every 10,000 verifications, approximately 200-300 results may be inaccurate. For newsletter lists and general marketing lists where 97-98% accuracy is sufficient, this is acceptable. For high-stakes cold outbound where every bounce damages domain reputation, the 1-2% accuracy gap vs. premium tools may justify the higher cost of ZeroBounce or Bouncer.
