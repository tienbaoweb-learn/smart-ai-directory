## Overview

Offrs is a real estate platform that uses a patented predictive algorithm and more than 250 data points — property tax records, demographic shifts, home equity changes, life-event signals, and web behavior — to score homeowners in a target market by their likelihood of listing their home for sale within the next year. Rather than prospecting an entire neighborhood at random, agents can focus effort on the subset of homeowners the model flags as most likely to sell.

For agents and brokerages, this reframes prospecting from a volume game into a targeting problem: the platform claims over 70% accuracy in predicting likely sellers, which — if borne out in a given market — meaningfully changes the math on cold outreach.

This review evaluates Offrs based on its prediction approach, campaign tools, and practical fit for agents focused on listing acquisition heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Predictive Seller Scoring

Offrs applies machine learning across a wide range of public and proprietary data to score every homeowner in a target market by their probability of listing within the next 12 months, rather than treating all homeowners as equally likely prospects.

### Territory Builder

Agents can claim a defined geographic territory, giving them an exclusive farming area where predicted seller leads are surfaced specifically to them rather than shared broadly across competitors.

### Automated Outreach Campaigns

Beyond identifying likely sellers, Offrs includes tools to launch online campaigns — including ads and email sequences — that introduce an agent's brand to predicted sellers automatically, rather than requiring fully manual outreach.

### Lead Feed & Capture Tools

Predicted sellers who engage are routed through lead capture tools into a feed agents can work directly, connecting the prediction layer to an actual, actionable pipeline.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Patented predictive algorithm scores homeowners by likelihood to sell
- ✅ Uses over 250 data points spanning property, demographic, and behavioral signals
- ✅ Includes built-in campaign tools to reach predicted sellers automatically
- ✅ Territory-based model gives agents a defined, exclusive farming area

## Cons

- ❌ Exclusive territory model means availability depends on your specific market
- ❌ Prediction accuracy, while strong, is not a guarantee for any individual homeowner
- ❌ Requires consistent follow-up effort to convert predicted leads into listings
- ❌ Cost is ongoing and tied to maintaining a territory, not a one-time purchase

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Offrs is the right tool if you:

- **Want to prioritize prospecting effort** rather than treating every homeowner equally
- **Are building a long-term farming strategy** in a specific geographic territory
- **Compete against larger, better-resourced firms** and need a data-driven edge
- **Are willing to follow up consistently** on leads the system surfaces

Offrs is **not** the right tool if you:

- Operate in a market where your desired territory is already claimed by another agent
- Want a one-time purchase rather than an ongoing territory subscription
- Expect every predicted lead to convert without sustained outreach effort

---

## Alternatives to Consider

Offrs is strong for predictive seller lead generation, but it is not the only option:

- **Zillow Showcase** — focused on presenting active listings beautifully rather than predicting future sellers
- **Grape Leads** — different focus, targeting local businesses without websites rather than home sellers
- **Leader CRM** — better if your core need is pipeline and contact management rather than lead prediction
- **Homesage AI** — focused on property investment and valuation analysis rather than seller prediction
- **Geo Targetly** — useful for geo-personalizing your own marketing rather than predicting sellers

---

## Final Verdict

Offrs earns its place for agents and brokerages who want to bring data-driven targeting to a traditionally volume-based prospecting game. Scoring an entire market by seller likelihood — rather than treating every homeowner as an equal prospect — is a meaningfully different approach to farming.

The territory-based model means the value proposition depends heavily on your specific market and competition for that territory, and consistent follow-up remains essential to actually converting predicted leads.

**Our recommendation:** Check territory availability in your specific market first, and go in with a clear follow-up plan for predicted leads rather than expecting the prediction alone to generate listings.
