## Overview

Perspective AI replaces the standard contact form on an agent or brokerage website with an AI-driven conversation. Instead of asking a stranger to fill in a dozen fields before they get any value, it greets the prospect, asks an open question, and lets them respond in their own words.

Behind that conversation, it runs the kind of qualifying interview a strong buyer's agent would — capturing budget, timeline, location, financing status, and motivation — then routes the structured insight to the CRM and flags hot leads instantly. The pitch is simple: the contact form is where most leads leak, so replace it with something that actually qualifies.

This review evaluates Perspective AI on its lead capture, qualification, and fit for agents and brokerages in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Conversational Lead Capture

Perspective AI swaps the static form for an adaptive AI interview that asks the right follow-ups and captures intent in the prospect's own words.

### Real-Time Qualification

It qualifies budget, timeline, location, financing, and motivation up front, so agents know who's worth calling first.

### CRM Routing

Structured lead insight is routed to the agent's CRM in real time, with hot leads flagged for immediate follow-up.

### Usage-Based Model

Priced around conversation volume rather than seats, which can scale more gently for solo agents than per-seat CRMs.

## Who Is It Best For?

Perspective AI is the right tool if you:

- **Have website traffic** you want to convert into qualified leads
- **Lose leads at the contact-form step**
- **Want intent captured** before the first human touch
- **Prefer conversation-volume pricing** over per-seat CRMs

Perspective AI is **not** the right tool if you:

- Have little website traffic to feed it
- Need a full CRM and long-term nurture rather than capture
- Want a flat, published price before evaluating

## Final Verdict

Perspective AI earns its place for agents and brokerages whose websites get traffic but lose it at the contact form. Replacing that form with a real qualifying conversation — and routing structured intent to the CRM — targets the single leakiest point in the funnel.

It's a capture-and-qualify tool, not a CRM or long-term nurture engine, so it works best alongside one.
