## Overview

Doxel is an AI-powered construction monitoring platform that uses autonomous robots equipped with LiDAR scanners and cameras to capture as-built site conditions, then compares that data against BIM models to identify progress deviations, quality issues, and productivity trends in near real time.

Rather than relying on manual site walks and subjective progress reporting, Doxel gives project teams quantitative data on what has been built, how it compares to the model, and how labor productivity is tracking against the schedule. For owners and general contractors on large commercial projects, this shifts progress monitoring from an opinion-based activity to a data-driven one.

This review evaluates Doxel based on its monitoring capabilities, BIM integration, and practical fit for construction project teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Autonomous Site Scanning

Doxel deploys autonomous robots that navigate construction sites independently, capturing LiDAR point clouds and visual data across large floor areas without requiring a technician to manually scan. This makes regular, comprehensive site captures operationally practical on large projects.

### AI BIM Comparison

Captured scan data is automatically compared against the project's BIM model, with AI identifying where as-built conditions deviate from the design — flagging geometry misalignments, missing work, and potential quality issues for review by the project team.

### Schedule and Productivity Analytics

Beyond quality, Doxel tracks labor productivity by measuring how much work is being installed each day against the project schedule. This gives superintendents and project managers an objective data source for identifying productivity trends and schedule risk before they become critical path issues.

### Early Rework Detection

By identifying deviations from the BIM model early — rather than during a formal inspection — Doxel reduces the cost of rework. Catching a coordination error when it can still be corrected before adjacent work is installed is significantly cheaper than finding it after enclosure.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Autonomous robots capture LiDAR and visual data across the site without manual scanning
- ✅ AI compares as-built conditions to BIM models to identify deviations in near real time
- ✅ Tracks labor productivity and schedule progress with quantitative data rather than subjective estimates
- ✅ Reduces rework costs by catching quality and coordination issues earlier in construction

## Cons

- ❌ Hardware deployment and integration adds complexity versus software-only solutions
- ❌ Primarily suited to large commercial projects where the ROI justifies the implementation cost
- ❌ Data collection frequency depends on robot scheduling rather than continuous monitoring

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Doxel is the right tool if you:

- **Manage large commercial construction projects** with detailed BIM models and high rework cost exposure
- **Need objective progress data** rather than relying on superintendent reports and schedule estimates
- **Want to reduce rework costs** through earlier detection of quality and coordination deviations
- **Have owner or GC accountability** for project cost and schedule performance

Doxel is **not** the right tool if you:

- Work on small or residential projects where manual monitoring is sufficient
- Don't have BIM models to serve as the comparison baseline
- Need a software-only solution without hardware deployment requirements

## Final Verdict

Doxel represents a meaningful advance in construction quality control and progress monitoring — replacing subjective site walk reporting with AI-analyzed scan data that can objectively compare what has been built against what was designed. For large commercial projects where rework costs and schedule delays are significant financial risks, this shift from opinion to data is valuable.

The implementation overhead is real, and the tool is not suited to smaller projects or teams without BIM infrastructure. But for the right project scale, Doxel's early deviation detection capability pays for itself by catching expensive coordination issues before they become rework.

**Our recommendation:** Evaluate Doxel's ROI against your last two or three projects' rework costs. If rework and late-stage quality issues were a meaningful cost on those projects, Doxel's early detection value is worth the implementation investment.
