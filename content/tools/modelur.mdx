## Overview

Modelur is a parametric urban design tool that runs inside SketchUp, letting architects and urban designers create and test massing alternatives quickly. Instead of modeling buildings manually, users work with parametric buildings and get live feedback on the metrics that drive urban design.

Its core value is speed with compliance: as you adjust massing, Modelur tracks FAR, apartment counts, parking needs, and zoning rules in real time, warning when a design breaks local regulations. For urban-scale feasibility, that turns a slow manual loop into rapid iteration.

This review evaluates Modelur on its parametric workflow, zoning intelligence, and fit for urban design in SketchUp in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Parametric Massing

One-click massing generation and parametric buildings let designers create and revise urban schemes several times faster than manual modeling.

### Live Performance Indicators

Modelur tracks FAR, apartments, and parking in real time, so the impact of a design change is visible immediately.

### Interactive 3D Zoning

The tool adapts buildings to local zoning and warns when designs don't comply — for example, when buildings are too tall or too close together.

### SketchUp and GIS Integration

Modelur works inside SketchUp and supports GIS via GeoJSON import/export, plus LiveSync with Excel on Windows.

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Modelur is the right tool if you:

- **Already work in SketchUp** and want parametric urban massing
- **Do feasibility and massing studies** on urban sites
- **Need live FAR, parking, and zoning metrics** while modeling
- **Work on projects where zoning** drives the massing

Modelur is **not** the right tool if you:

- Don't use SketchUp and don't want to adopt it
- Need detailed building design, BIM, or rendering
- Work on single small buildings where parametric urban tools add little

## Final Verdict

Modelur earns its place for architects and urban designers who live in SketchUp and need to test massing against real zoning and feasibility metrics fast. The combination of parametric buildings and live FAR, parking, and zoning feedback makes early urban design iteration much quicker.

It's a focused tool: a SketchUp plugin for massing and feasibility, not a design, BIM, or rendering platform.
