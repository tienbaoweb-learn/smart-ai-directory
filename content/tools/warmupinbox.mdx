## Overview

Email deliverability is the foundation of every outreach campaign. With email providers like Google and Yahoo enforcing strict spam complaint thresholds in 2026, a new sending domain without warmup will land in spam — not inbox — regardless of how good the email copy is.

Email warmup tools solve this by automatically exchanging emails with a network of real inboxes, building your domain's sending reputation gradually before you launch campaigns. Warmup Inbox does exactly this, at the lowest price point in the dedicated warmup category.

At $15/inbox/month, it is the budget choice for teams that need standalone warmup without the added cost of an outreach platform.

---

## Key Features

### Automated Email Warmup Network

Warmup Inbox connects your email account to a network of 30,000+ real, active inboxes. The platform automatically sends and receives emails between your account and the network — marking emails as important, moving them from spam to inbox, and generating the engagement signals that email providers use to assess sender reputation.

Setup takes minutes: connect your email account via SMTP, IMAP, or OAuth (for Gmail and Outlook), and the warmup process starts automatically.

### ESP-Specific Warmup

Target warmup emails specifically toward Gmail inboxes or Outlook inboxes depending on where your prospects primarily receive email. This is a meaningful differentiator — warming up primarily against Gmail accounts builds Gmail reputation, which translates to better inbox placement when your actual campaign goes to Gmail recipients.

### Language-Specific Warmup

For teams running multilingual outreach campaigns, Warmup Inbox supports 12 languages for warmup email content. Emails in the local language look more natural to email provider filters — relevant for architecture firms, real estate agencies, or construction companies targeting clients in non-English markets.

### Built-In Spam and Blacklist Monitoring

Warmup Inbox monitors your domain and IP for blacklist appearances and spam folder placement. Alerts notify you before deliverability problems compound into campaign-level damage.

### Cold Email Sequencer

Beyond warmup, Warmup Inbox includes a basic cold email sequencer with contact list verification — allowing simple outreach campaigns to be run from the same platform. This is a secondary feature, not the core product, but useful for teams that do not need a full outreach platform.

### Email Spam Checker (Free)

A free email spam checker tool is included, allowing you to test individual emails against common spam filter criteria before sending campaigns.

## How Long Does Warmup Take?

Most email deliverability experts recommend 2-4 weeks of warmup before launching cold outreach campaigns. Rushing warmup — starting campaigns too early — can backfire by creating sudden volume spikes that trigger spam filters.

Users report measurable improvements in email reputation within the first week, with open rate increases of over 30% within two months of consistent warmup.

## Use Cases by Industry

### Architecture & Construction

Architecture firms and construction companies running targeted email outreach to developers, property owners, or procurement teams need inbox placement — not spam folders. New sending domains for business development outreach benefit from 3-4 weeks of warmup before campaign launch.

### Real Estate

Real estate agents and agencies doing cold email prospecting benefit from warmup on new domains and sending addresses. Higher inbox placement rates directly translate to more responses from property owners, investors, and buyers.

## Who Is It Best For?

Warmup Inbox is the right fit if you:

- Need standalone email warmup at the lowest possible cost
- Are warming 1-5 inboxes and per-inbox pricing is manageable
- Use Gmail, Outlook, or custom SMTP for outreach (not already on Instantly/Smartlead)
- Need language-specific warmup for multilingual campaigns

It is **not** the right fit if you:

- Are already on an outreach platform that includes free warmup (Instantly, Smartlead)
- Need to warm 10+ inboxes — per-inbox pricing becomes expensive at scale
- Need advanced deliverability analytics and inbox placement testing
