## Overview

DealCheck lets real estate investors and agents analyze any investment property in seconds — entering purchase price, financing terms, closing costs, rehab budget, rent roll, and estimated expenses to instantly see cash flow, cap rate, ROI, and dozens of other metrics. It covers a wide range of deal types: single-family and multi-family rentals, commercial properties, BRRRRs, Airbnb investments, house flips, and wholesale deals.

For real estate investors, the value is straightforward: the difference between a good deal and a bad one often comes down to numbers that are easy to get wrong when calculated manually or under time pressure — and DealCheck's mobile apps mirror the web interface closely enough to run a full analysis during an actual property tour.

This review evaluates DealCheck based on its analysis depth, metric accuracy, and practical fit for on-the-go investment decision-making heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Multi-Deal-Type Analysis

DealCheck supports analyzing single-family and multi-family rentals, commercial properties, BRRRRs, Airbnbs, house flips, and wholesale deals, covering most of the common investment strategies in one platform.

### Comprehensive Financial Metrics

Users get instant access to dozens of metrics — cap rate, cash-on-cash return, return on equity, ROI, IRR, rent-to-value, gross rent multiplier, and break-even ratios — rather than just a basic cash flow estimate.

### Long-Term Projections

Beyond a single snapshot, DealCheck lets investors explore long-term cash flow projections for rentals and BRRRRs, and profit projections for flips, supporting decisions that look beyond just the first year.

### Mobile-Synced Analysis

The iOS and Android apps closely mirror the web interface, letting investors and agents run a full property analysis on their phone during a property tour rather than waiting until they're back at a desk.

## Pros & Cons

- ✅ Analyzes single-family, multi-family, commercial, BRRRR, Airbnb, flip, and wholesale deals
- ✅ Instantly calculates cash flow, cap rate, ROI, IRR, and dozens of other metrics
- ✅ Mobile apps mirror the web interface for on-the-go analysis during property tours

## Cons

- ❌ Output quality depends entirely on the accuracy of inputs — rent, expenses, rehab costs
- ❌ Long-term projections are estimates and can't account for unpredictable market shifts
- ❌ Covers financial analysis specifically, not property condition or legal due diligence
- ❌ More advanced multi-family and commercial features may need a paid plan

---

## Who Is It Best For?

DealCheck is the right tool if you:

- **Evaluate multiple investment properties** and need fast, consistent financial analysis
- **Want to run numbers during a property tour** rather than waiting until later
- **Invest across different strategies** — rentals, flips, BRRRRs, wholesale — and want one tool for all of them
- **Need to communicate deal metrics clearly** to partners, lenders, or buyers

DealCheck is **not** the right tool if you:

- Need property condition assessment or legal due diligence, not just financial analysis
- Are working with unreliable rent, expense, or rehab cost estimates that undermine the output
- Want a tool that also handles property history research like deaths or stigmatized events

## Alternatives to Consider

DealCheck is strong for investment property financial analysis, but it is not the only option:

- **Homesage AI** — comparable predictive investment scoring with a different data and AI approach
- **FutureLot** — better for zoning and ADU buildability rather than financial deal analysis
- **DiedInHouse.com** — relevant for property history research rather than financial metrics
- **Offrs** — useful for finding likely sellers rather than analyzing a specific deal's numbers
- **Zillow Showcase** — relevant for listing presentation rather than investment analysis

## Final Verdict

DealCheck earns its place as a thorough, mobile-friendly way to run the numbers on a potential investment property before committing. Covering financial analysis across nearly every common investment strategy in one tool — with mobile apps that genuinely work during a property tour — removes a real source of friction and error from fast-moving deal evaluation.

The analysis is only as good as the inputs — careful, realistic estimates of rent, expenses, and rehab costs matter more than the tool itself for getting an accurate picture of a deal.

**Our recommendation:** Run a deal you've already closed or know well through DealCheck first, to calibrate how closely its projections matched reality before relying on it heavily for new investment decisions.
