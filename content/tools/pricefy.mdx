## Overview

Pricing is one of the most impactful levers in eCommerce — and one of the most time-consuming to manage manually. A competitor drops their price on a key product category on a Tuesday afternoon. By the time your team notices on Thursday, you have lost two days of potential sales to a price disadvantage that an automated system would have caught in minutes.

Pricefy automates this entire process. Trusted by 10,000+ eCommerce businesses, it monitors competitor prices across any website or marketplace, matches products automatically using AI, and triggers repricing rules to keep your prices competitive — all from a single dashboard.

For furniture retailers, interior design product brands, and any business selling physical products in competitive online markets, Pricefy provides the competitive intelligence and automation that previously required dedicated pricing analysts.

---

## Key Features

### AI Product Automatch

The core technical challenge in competitor price monitoring is matching your product to the same product on a competitor's site — when titles, SKUs, and attributes often differ. Pricefy's AI automatch handles this automatically.

The system first tries barcode matching (EAN, GTIN, UPC). When barcode data is unavailable, it falls back to AI and machine learning that compares visual and text attributes to identify the same product. Manual override and correction is always available when the AI match is incorrect.

This eliminates the hours of manual product mapping that competitor monitoring traditionally requires.

### Real-Time Competitor Monitoring

Upload your product catalog and Pricefy finds and monitors every competitor automatically. Daily price updates with 99% scraping accuracy cover any website — not just major marketplaces. Anti-detection scraping technology mimics regular browser behavior, preventing competitors from identifying or blocking monitoring activity.

Track every product variant (size, color, storage) as a separate data point. Compare at the variant level rather than just the parent product — critical for furniture businesses where a sofa in different fabric options may be priced differently across competitors.

### Marketplace Analysis

Track competitor pricing on Amazon, eBay, and Google Shopping alongside their own website pricing. For furniture retailers and interior design brands selling across multiple channels, understanding how competitors price across channels — including whether they discount on Amazon vs. their own site — is essential intelligence.

### Dynamic Repricing

Set automated repricing rules that adjust your prices in response to competitor movements. Rules can be configured to match the lowest competitor, undercut by a fixed amount or percentage, maintain a price premium above cheapest, or protect minimum margin thresholds.

Repricing is available from the Pro plan and integrates with major eCommerce platforms to apply price changes automatically.

### Instant Alerts

Receive notifications via email or Slack the moment a competitor changes their price, goes out of stock, or modifies a product listing. React immediately to competitive movements rather than discovering them in next week's report.

### MAP/MSRP Monitoring

For brands distributing through reseller networks, Pricefy monitors whether resellers are pricing at or above your Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) or Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP). Catch MAP violations before they damage your brand positioning or create channel conflict. Available on Business plan.

### Pricing Intelligence Dashboards

Real-time dashboards show your market position against competitors: which products are priced higher, lower, or matching, with historical trend charts showing how competitor prices have evolved over time. Custom reports can be scheduled for automated delivery to your team.

## Use Cases by Industry

### Furniture Retail

Furniture is a high-competition, price-sensitive category where consumers compare prices across multiple retailers before purchasing. Pricefy enables furniture retailers to monitor competitor pricing across key product categories daily, identify when competitors discount seasonal lines, and adjust pricing automatically to maintain competitive positioning without eroding margins.

MAP monitoring is particularly valuable for furniture brands distributing through retail networks — catching resellers discounting below MAP before it damages brand value.

### Interior Design Products

Interior design product brands selling through multiple channels (own website, Amazon, boutique retailers) need visibility into how their products are priced across the ecosystem. Pricefy's reseller monitoring maps every seller of your products online, identifying unauthorized resellers and pricing discrepancies that affect brand positioning.

## Who Is It Best For?

Pricefy is the right fit if you:

- Sell physical products in competitive online markets
- Currently monitor competitor prices manually or not at all
- Need dynamic repricing to stay competitive without constant manual intervention
- Distribute through resellers and need MAP/MSRP compliance monitoring
- Run a Shopify, WooCommerce, or Magento store

It is **not** the right fit if you:

- Sell pure services or digital products where competitor pricing monitoring is less relevant

- Need enterprise-level pricing strategy consulting alongside the monitoring tool
