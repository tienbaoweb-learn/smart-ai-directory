## Overview

Cometly is a marketing attribution platform built to answer a question many businesses struggle with: which specific ad campaigns actually lead to revenue, not just clicks or form fills. By connecting ad spend data with downstream lead and sales outcomes, Cometly gives a clearer picture of true return on ad spend across channels.

This matters most for businesses with a gap between the initial ad click and the final sale — a common pattern in real estate, construction, and high-ticket furniture sales, where a lead might take weeks or months to convert. Without proper attribution, it's easy to misallocate ad budget toward channels that generate cheap leads but few actual closed deals.

This review evaluates Cometly based on its attribution accuracy, integration depth, and practical value for businesses with longer sales cycles heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Closed-Loop Attribution

Cometly connects ad click data through to actual lead and revenue outcomes, letting businesses see which campaigns, ads, or keywords are responsible for closed deals — not just initial engagement.

### Ad Platform Integrations

The platform integrates with major ad platforms, syncing conversion and revenue data back to inform bidding and budget decisions based on real outcomes rather than surface-level metrics.

### CRM & Sales Data Connection

By connecting to CRM or sales data, Cometly can trace a lead's full journey from first ad interaction to closed sale, which is essential for businesses where the sales cycle spans multiple touchpoints over time.

### Revenue-Based Reporting

Rather than reporting on clicks and impressions alone, Cometly's dashboards focus on revenue and deal outcomes, making it easier to communicate marketing ROI in terms leadership actually cares about.

- ✅ Useful for businesses with longer sales cycles between lead and close

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Cometly is the right tool if you:

- **Run paid ad campaigns** and need to know which ones actually drive revenue, not just leads
- **Have a sales cycle with a delay** between the initial lead and the closed deal
- **Need to justify ad spend** to leadership with revenue-based reporting rather than vanity metrics
- **Already use a CRM** that can be connected to track the full lead-to-revenue journey

Cometly is **not** the right tool if you:

- Have minimal or no paid advertising spend to attribute
- Run a very simple, single-channel marketing operation where attribution complexity isn't needed
- Don't have a CRM or sales data system to connect for closed-loop tracking

---

## Alternatives to Consider

Cometly is strong for closed-loop ad attribution, but it is not the only option:

- **Geo Targetly** — focused on geo-personalized landing pages rather than attribution reporting
- **SearchAtlas** — better if your primary channel is organic SEO rather than paid ads
- **AdTurbo** — focused on automating ad management and bidding rather than attribution analysis
- **Involve.me** — useful for the lead capture side rather than tracking attribution to revenue
- **Grape Leads** — relevant for prospecting rather than measuring existing campaign performance

---

## Final Verdict

Cometly earns its place for businesses that are already spending on paid ads but lack visibility into what's actually driving revenue versus just generating cheap, low-quality leads. The closed-loop attribution approach directly addresses one of the most common and costly blind spots in digital marketing for long-sales-cycle industries.

The setup investment is real — connecting ad accounts and CRM data properly takes some effort — but the payoff is clearer, revenue-based decision-making on where ad budget actually belongs.

**Our recommendation:** Connect your highest-spend ad channel and CRM first to validate attribution accuracy before rolling Cometly out across your full marketing stack.
