## Overview

Insightful (formerly known as Workpuls) has evolved into a premier workforce analytics and employee monitoring platform. Moving beyond traditional time tracking, Insightful provides deep, data-driven visibility into how work is actually executed across distributed, hybrid, and office-based teams. By capturing activity data in the background, it helps organizations spot workflow bottlenecks, calculate accurate billable hours, and optimize employee productivity without micromanagement.

For professionals in **Architecture, Construction, Interior Design, and Real Estate**, time is literally money. From the initial schematic design phases in CAD to client presentations and site visits, understanding where time goes is critical to maintaining project profitability. Insightful caters specifically to these needs by allowing managers to tie tracked time directly to specific projects, phases, and cost centers, turning raw workforce data into actionable operational insights.

---

## Key Features

### Automated Time Tracking & Timesheets
Insightful removes the friction of manual time entry. The software automatically tracks computer activity and maps it to specific apps and websites, distinguishing between active and idle time. At the end of the day or week, the platform generates accurate timesheets automatically, which managers can easily review, approve, and export for payroll or client invoicing.

### Deep Productivity Analytics
Rather than just logging hours, Insightful categorizes apps and websites as productive, unproductive, or neutral based on an employee's specific role. A 3D rendering software might be productive for an architect, but a distraction for an administrative assistant. This granularity allows business leaders to benchmark productive output, pinpoint wasted effort, and intervene before key performance indicators decline.

### Project and Task Tracking
For project-based industries like construction and interior design, Insightful is a game-changer. Employees can log their time against specific projects or tasks (e.g., "Design Development" vs. "Construction Documents"). This feature ensures budgets are kept in check, prevents scope creep, and guarantees that all billable hours are accurately captured and invoiced to clients.

### Employee Monitoring & Stealth Mode
To ensure accountability, Insightful offers optional screenshot capture and stealth mode monitoring. While visible monitoring encourages self-accountability, stealth mode allows administrators to monitor company-owned devices without interrupting user workflows. This feature is paired with insider threat detection to secure sensitive client data and architectural intellectual property.

### Industry-Leading Security
When handling proprietary architectural designs and confidential real estate data, security is paramount. Insightful boasts enterprise-grade security with multi-certificate compliance, including HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR, ensuring that all monitored data is encrypted and kept private.

## Who Is It Best For?

Insightful is the right tool if you:

- **Run an architecture or interior design firm** needing to track hours spent precisely on CAD, 3D modeling, and design revisions.
- **Manage construction projects** and need to track back-office productivity, allocate workloads, and ensure accurate task budgeting.
- **Operate a real estate agency** with remote agents and need visibility into tool utilization and daily workflow efficiency.
- **Require enterprise-grade security** and data warehousing capabilities (like Snowflake or BigQuery integrations) to process workforce data at scale.

Insightful is **not** the right tool if you:

- Run a very small team of independent freelancers where basic, manual stop-watch timers (like Toggl) are sufficient.
- Rely heavily on mobile-first field workers who need robust mobile time-tracking features over desktop analytics.
- Dislike the concept of background employee monitoring and screen captures due to company culture preferences.

## Alternatives to Consider

If Insightful doesn't perfectly align with your workflow, consider these top alternatives:

- **Hubstaff** — better for teams heavily reliant on mobile GPS tracking for field and construction workers.
- **Clockify** — a strong, budget-friendly alternative focusing purely on simple time and timesheet management without deep monitoring.
- **Time Doctor** — offers very similar productivity tracking and screenshots but with different reporting interfaces.
- **Toggl Track** — the gold standard for manual, frictionless time tracking favored by solo designers and freelancers.
- **Apploye** — an excellent solution for architects and engineers offering both time tracking and robust payroll/invoicing features.
