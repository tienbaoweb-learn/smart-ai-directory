## Overview

Most content creators build their audience on platforms they do not own. Podcasters on Spotify. Course creators on Udemy. Educators on YouTube. When the algorithm changes, the platform changes its terms, or a competing creator gets promoted instead, they have limited recourse.

Audiorista is built for creators who want to own their audience. It is a white-label content platform that lets you launch your own branded media app — for audio, video, ebooks, and courses — under your name, with your branding, on your terms. Your subscribers are your data. Your revenue is not shared with a platform marketplace. Your app exists on iOS and Android without you writing a single line of code.

For architecture educators, construction training providers, real estate coaches, and publishers serving professional audiences, this ownership model is the fundamental differentiator from building on third-party platforms.

---

## Key Features

### White-Label Branded App

Your content, your brand. Audiorista builds and maintains iOS and Android apps under your branding — your logo, your color scheme, your app name in the App Store and Google Play. Users who download your app see your brand at every touchpoint, not Audiorista's.

This is the core value proposition: the technical complexity of app development (typically $50,000-$200,000+ for a custom app) is replaced by a monthly subscription that delivers the same branded experience.

### Multi-Format Content Delivery

Audiorista supports four content types in a single platform:

**Audio:** Podcasts, audiobooks, guided meditations, coaching recordings, training modules — delivered with offline access and HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) for smooth, secure playback.

**Video:** Training videos, webinars, recorded courses, tutorials — with the same offline and streaming capabilities as audio.

**Ebooks:** Structured reading content with bookmarking, progress tracking, and reading speed controls.

**Courses:** Structured learning pathways combining audio, video, ebook, and interactive elements into curriculum sequences with progress tracking.

For architecture educators delivering CPD content, or construction training providers covering safety and compliance, the ability to combine formats in one platform — rather than managing separate tools for audio, video, and text — reduces operational overhead significantly.

### Monetization Flexibility

Configure subscription models, paywalls for individual content pieces, and one-time purchase options. Integration with iOS, Android, and leading ecommerce solutions handles payment processing. Revenue flows to you — Audiorista does not take a platform percentage of content sales beyond the subscription fee.

This contrasts with Udemy (50% revenue share) or Spotify (streaming royalties) where the platform takes a significant portion of creator revenue.

### Custom Onboarding and User Experience

Design the onboarding flow new subscribers experience when they download your app: welcome screens, content categories, recommended starting points. The browsing and playback experience is fully configurable to match your brand's design standards.

### Offline Access and HLS Streaming

Subscribers can download content for offline listening and viewing — critical for professionals who consume training content during commutes, travel, or on construction sites without reliable connectivity. HLS streaming ensures smooth playback adapts to connection quality when online.

### Multi-Language Localization

Configure your app in multiple languages — supporting international audiences without maintaining separate apps per market. Relevant for architecture and construction training providers serving global markets.

## Use Cases by Industry

### Architecture and Construction Education

Architecture CPD (Continuing Professional Development) providers and construction training companies have professional audiences who expect premium content experiences. A branded app positions training content as a professional product rather than a YouTube channel or email newsletter — improving perceived value, subscriber retention, and pricing power.

Construction safety training, in particular, benefits from offline download capability: field workers consuming compliance training in areas without reliable mobile connectivity.

### Real Estate Coaching

Real estate coaches and educators running subscription training programs benefit from the branded app model: subscribers who download your app engage more consistently than those relying on email links to a website. Push notifications keep subscribers engaged with new content releases without platform algorithm dependency.

### Publishers and Content Companies

Publishers producing professional content — industry analysis, research reports, expert interviews — can launch a branded subscription app that positions them as a media brand rather than just a website. Architecture and construction trade publishers are a natural fit.

## Who Is It Best For?

Audiorista is the right fit if you:

- Have existing content (or are building content) and want to distribute it under your own brand
- Run a subscription content business or want to launch one
- Want iOS and Android app presence without custom development cost
- Value audience ownership over platform reach
- Serve a professional audience that expects premium branded experiences

It is **not** the right fit if you:

- Are just starting content creation and do not yet have an audience
- Need a platform marketplace to discover new subscribers (Audiorista is for owned audiences)
- Are a hobby creator — the $60/month entry point requires monetization to be viable
