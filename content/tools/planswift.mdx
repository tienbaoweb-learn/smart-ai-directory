## Overview

PlanSwift is construction takeoff and estimating software built around a visual, point-and-click interface — users drag and drop parts or assemblies directly onto a digitized blueprint to gather quantities and build an estimate, rather than measuring plans by hand or manually entering quantities into a spreadsheet. Its newer AI capability, Takeoff Boost, gives estimators an automated first pass on scaling and project setup so review time focuses on checking and pricing rather than raw measurement.

For contractors and estimators producing takeoffs and bids regularly, this matters because manual measurement is one of the slowest, most error-prone parts of pre-construction — a missed quantity or a scaling mistake can throw off an entire bid, and PlanSwift's digitized, click-based workflow reduces both the time and the risk of that manual process.

This review evaluates PlanSwift based on its takeoff accuracy, AI-assisted features, and practical fit for contractors and estimators heading into 2026.

---

## Key Features

### AI-Assisted Takeoff (Takeoff Boost)

Takeoff Boost applies AI to scaling, takeoff, and project setup, giving estimators an automated first pass on a set of plans so they can focus their time on reviewing and pricing rather than raw manual measurement.

### Visual Point-and-Click Takeoff

Users drag and drop individual parts or full assemblies directly onto a digitized blueprint to gather quantity takeoffs, with linears, unit counts, square footage, pitches, and angles measurable in seconds with a single click.

### Aerial & Multi-Format Plan Support

PlanSwift integrates with Google Maps for aerial takeoffs of roofing, asphalt, or land areas, and supports common plan formats — PDF, DWG, DXF, TIF, JPG — including plans imported directly from online plan rooms.

### Direct Excel Integration

Takeoff quantities can be sent directly into existing Microsoft Excel estimating spreadsheets, fitting into an estimator's established pricing workflow rather than requiring a switch to a new estimating format.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Takeoff Boost gives an AI-assisted first pass on scaling and takeoff before manual review
- ✅ Visual point-and-click interface drops assemblies directly onto a digitized blueprint
- ✅ Measures linears, unit counts, square footage, pitches, and angles in a single click
- ✅ Supports common plan formats (PDF, DWG, DXF, TIF) and sends quantities straight to Excel

## Cons

- ❌ AI-assisted takeoff still needs an estimator's review before pricing goes out the door
- ❌ Best value comes with a real, recurring volume of takeoffs to justify the license cost
- ❌ Learning assemblies and templates well takes some upfront setup time
- ❌ Primarily a Windows-based desktop tool rather than a lightweight browser app

---

## Who Is It Best For?

PlanSwift is the right tool if you:

- **Produce takeoffs and estimates regularly** across multiple trades or project types
- **Want an AI first pass on scaling and measurement** rather than doing it entirely by hand
- **Already estimate in Excel** and want takeoff quantities to flow directly into that workflow
- **Work with digital plans** in standard formats like PDF, DWG, or DXF

PlanSwift is **not** the right tool if you:

- Only bid the occasional project where manual takeoff is fast enough already
- Need a browser-first, cross-platform tool rather than a Windows desktop application
- Want fully automated estimating without any estimator review before pricing

---

## Alternatives to Consider

- **Handoff** — better if you want AI estimating combined with CRM and client pipeline management
- **Buildots** — focused on automated progress tracking during construction, not pre-bid takeoffs
- **TestFit** — relevant earlier, for site feasibility and massing rather than detailed takeoffs
- **Procore AI** — better suited for larger commercial project management beyond estimating alone
- **PlanGrid** — narrower focus on document and field management, now in maintenance mode

---

## Final Verdict

PlanSwift earns its place for contractors and estimators who produce takeoffs regularly and want to cut the time and error risk out of manual measurement. The combination of a visual, click-based takeoff workflow and the Takeoff Boost AI first pass addresses the most tedious part of pre-construction estimating without removing the estimator's final judgment on pricing.

For occasional bidders, the license investment may not be justified — PlanSwift delivers the most value for teams estimating consistently across multiple projects and trades.

**Our recommendation:** Run Takeoff Boost on a project you've already estimated manually to compare its automated first pass against your known numbers before relying on it for a live bid.
