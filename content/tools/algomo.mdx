## Overview

Customer support is expensive to scale. Every new customer who asks "where is my order?" or "do you deliver to my area?" requires a human agent's time — unless an AI can handle it first. Algomo is built to handle it first.

Using ChatGPT technology, Algomo's AI bot responds to repetitive customer queries automatically, escalates complex issues to live agents when needed, and translates conversations in real time for multilingual teams. The result: an 85% automation rate and a reported 10x increase in agent productivity — meaning your human team spends time on high-value interactions, not FAQ responses.

Setup takes 4 minutes. No coding required. Pricing starts at $9/month. For architecture firms, furniture retailers, and real estate agencies handling consistent inquiry volume, that math is compelling.

## Key Features

### ChatGPT-Powered AI Bot

Algomo's chatbot uses ChatGPT technology to respond to customer queries based on your business context: your products, your policies, your FAQs. Upload support documents or specify common questions and the bot learns your business — delivering accurate, on-brand responses without generic scripted replies.

The bot handles the full first-tier support layer: answering product questions, checking order status, providing shipping information, and directing visitors to the right resources. Complex or sensitive queries are escalated to live agents automatically with full conversation context preserved.

### AI Agents with Autonomous Decision-Making

Beyond simple FAQ responses, Algomo's AI agents take autonomous actions: making product recommendations based on visitor context, updating customers on order status in real time, and choosing which tools to use based on the query type. This is the step beyond basic chatbot functionality — AI that acts, not just responds.

For furniture eCommerce businesses, this means an AI that can recommend the right sofa based on room dimensions described by the customer, check real-time inventory, and confirm delivery timelines — without human intervention.

### Unified Inbox

All customer communications — AI bot conversations, live chats, and team messages — flow into a single unified inbox. Teams see the full conversation history, can jump in from any thread, and receive AI-generated suggestions for how to respond to complex queries.

The unified inbox integrates teams, channels, and data: support conversations alongside CRM context, previous purchase history, and AI-surfaced insights about what customers are asking most frequently.

### 100+ Language Support with Real-Time Translation

Algomo supports over 100 languages and provides automatic real-time translation for live chat conversations. A customer writing in Portuguese is understood; an agent replying in English is translated. For architecture firms, construction companies, and real estate agencies serving international clients, this eliminates language barriers without requiring multilingual staff.

### Lead Generation and Conversion Optimization

Beyond support, Algomo's AI bot actively contributes to conversion: identifying purchase intent signals, offering relevant products at the right moment, and capturing lead information for follow-up. For businesses where the website is a primary sales channel, this positions the chatbot as a revenue tool, not just a cost reduction tool.

### Analytics and Insights

AI-generated analytics surface trends and frequently asked questions — giving customer success and product teams visibility into what customers actually need. This data is actionable: common questions inform FAQ updates, product descriptions, and onboarding improvements.

## Use Cases by Industry

### Architecture & Construction

Architecture firms and construction companies receive consistent inquiry types: project consultation requests, timeline questions, portfolio inquiries, and service area questions. Algomo handles these first-tier queries automatically, collecting visitor information and routing qualified inquiries to the right team member — improving lead capture without adding administrative overhead.

Multilingual support is particularly relevant for architecture firms serving international developers or construction companies with diverse client bases.

### Real Estate

Real estate agencies fielding property inquiries benefit from Algomo's ability to qualify leads automatically: collecting preferred location, budget, property type, and timeline before routing to an agent. Routine queries (is this property still available, what are the viewing times) are handled by the AI — agents spend time on qualified buyers, not initial screening.

### Furniture eCommerce

Furniture retailers receive high volumes of pre-purchase queries: dimensions, material options, delivery timelines, assembly requirements. Algomo's AI handles these repetitively at scale, and the autonomous product recommendation capability surfaces relevant alternatives when a queried item is out of stock — turning potential lost sales into conversions.

## Who Is It Best For?

Algomo is the right fit if you:

- Handle consistent customer inquiry volume and want AI to manage first-tier responses
- Serve international customers needing multilingual support
- Want live chat and AI chatbot in one platform without managing two separate tools
- Need Shopify, HubSpot, or Zapier integration for your support workflow
- Are looking for a Zendesk or Intercom alternative at lower cost

It is **not** the right fit if you:

- Need enterprise-grade compliance, SSO, and governance features
- Require deep integration with complex CRM or ERP systems
- Have very low inquiry volume where the automation benefit does not justify the cost
