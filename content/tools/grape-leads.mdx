## Overview

Every web design agency faces the same prospecting challenge: finding businesses that need a website or need a better one — before a competitor does. Traditional B2B databases give you companies, but not the signal that matters: does this business have an online presence worth selling against?

Grape Leads was built to solve exactly this. It searches Google Maps (Google My Business) listings to identify local businesses without websites, or with Facebook pages listed in place of proper websites — the highest-quality signal that a business is ready for web design services. The result is actionable lead lists of businesses that need your services, not generic contact databases you have to manually qualify.

Created by a web agency owner who built the original tool for his own prospecting, Grape Leads is a focused, no-bloat tool for a specific and underserved use case in the digital agency world.

---

## Key Features

### No-Website Business Finder

The core feature: search Google Maps by business type and location, filtered to show only businesses without websites (or with Facebook pages as their "website"). Tell Grape Leads what business types you are targeting and where, and it returns a list of businesses that need your web design services.

For a web design agency targeting local restaurants, retail stores, or professional services in a specific city, this replaces hours of manual Google Maps browsing with automated, filtered results.

**Relevance to our industries:** Architecture firms and construction companies that serve local markets often encounter building contractors, tradespeople, and small construction suppliers who have no web presence. Grape Leads can identify these businesses for agencies serving these sectors.

### Outdated Website Detector

Beyond no-website businesses, Grape Leads performs batch analysis of business websites to identify underperforming sites — old design, poor mobile responsiveness, missing basic SEO elements. These are businesses that have a web presence but need a redesign or SEO overhaul.

This expands the prospecting universe beyond "no website" to "poor website" — a much larger market.

### SEO and Local SEO Analysis

Find and analyze small business websites for search and local SEO parameters. Generate detailed reports showing specific technical and content gaps that you can reference in your sales outreach — making the pitch specific rather than generic.

"Your site has no Google My Business connection, missing meta descriptions, and loads in 8 seconds on mobile" is a more compelling outreach angle than "do you need SEO services?"

### GMB Data Foundation

Grape Leads uses raw data from Google My Business listings — fresh, location-accurate, and regularly updated as businesses change their profiles. This is a meaningful advantage over purchased lead lists, which are often months or years out of date by the time they reach your desk.

### Credit-Based Pay-As-You-Go

Credits are used to initiate searches and retrieve individual results. One credit initiates a search; one additional credit per lead in the results. A search returning 20 no-website businesses costs 21 credits. This model keeps costs directly tied to the leads you actually retrieve — no subscription wastage.

## Use Cases by Industry

### Web Design Agencies Serving Architecture and Construction

Architecture practices, construction companies, and building trades are industries with notoriously uneven digital adoption. Many small and specialist firms — structural engineers, interior fit-out contractors, specialist trades — have no web presence or rely on a decade-old static site. Grape Leads identifies these businesses efficiently for web agencies targeting the built environment sector.

### Digital Agencies Targeting Furniture and Interior Design SMBs

Furniture retailers, interior designers, and home staging companies in regional markets often lack proper websites. Grape Leads surfaces these businesses by location and business type, giving digital agencies a qualified prospect list rather than cold outreach to businesses that may already have modern websites.

### Freelancers Building Their First Client Base

Individual web developers and designers who need to build a client roster from scratch benefit from Grape Leads' ability to generate hundreds of qualified local leads quickly. Rather than networking events and referrals alone, a freelancer can identify 50 local businesses needing a website and begin outreach systematically.

## Who Is It Best For?

Grape Leads is the right fit if you:

- Run a web design or SEO agency actively prospecting for new clients
- Are a freelance web developer targeting local businesses
- Want fresh, location-specific leads from Google Maps data rather than purchased lists
- Need to identify businesses by their absence of digital presence, not just their contact information

It is **not** the right fit if you:

- Need a general B2B lead generation tool across all industries and company types
- Are prospecting for enterprise businesses (Grape Leads focuses on local SMBs)
- Need contact information beyond what appears in Google My Business listings
- Are not in web design/digital services — the tool's value proposition is tied to the no-website signal

- **Manual Google Maps search** — free but not scalable; Grape Leads replaces this manual process at volume
