## Overview

Togal.AI is an AI-powered construction takeoff platform that uses artificial intelligence to automatically measure quantities from construction drawing PDFs — floor areas, wall lengths, door and window counts, and other measurable elements — in a fraction of the time required for manual takeoff. For construction estimators and general contractors, quantity takeoff is one of the most time-intensive steps in bid preparation, and Togal.AI directly compresses this bottleneck.

The platform was co-founded by Patrick Bet-David and has grown rapidly in the construction estimating market by demonstrating time savings on real bid workflows — particularly for commercial construction where drawing sets are large and manual takeoff is a multi-day process.

This review evaluates Togal.AI based on its takeoff accuracy, time savings, and practical fit for construction estimating teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### AI Quantity Takeoff from PDF Drawings

Togal.AI's core capability is analyzing uploaded construction drawing PDFs and automatically measuring quantities — floor areas by room type, linear footage of walls, counts of openings, and other measurable elements — using computer vision and AI trained on construction drawings. What might take an estimator hours to measure manually can be completed in minutes.

### Automatic Element Recognition

The AI identifies and categorizes building elements — distinguishing floor types, exterior vs. interior walls, different door and window types — and measures them separately, producing organized quantity outputs rather than requiring the estimator to sort and categorize afterward.

### Verification and Editing Layer

Togal.AI provides a visual review layer where estimators can check AI measurements against the original drawings, correct errors, and add manual measurements for elements the AI missed. This verification step is essential — AI measurement is a starting point, not an autonomous final output.

### Export to Estimating Software

Measured quantities export in formats compatible with standard estimating tools and spreadsheets, allowing estimators to feed Togal.AI output directly into their cost estimating workflow without re-entering numbers.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ AI performs quantity takeoffs from PDF drawings in a fraction of manual measurement time
- ✅ Automatically identifies and measures floor areas, walls, openings, and other elements
- ✅ Significant time reduction on bidding — particularly valuable for high-volume estimating teams
- ✅ Improves bid accuracy by reducing manual counting and measurement errors

## Cons

- ❌ Output accuracy requires human review — AI measurements need verification before use in final estimates
- ❌ Works best with clear, well-organized construction drawings — less effective on ambiguous plans
- ❌ Does not replace estimating judgment — it automates measurement, not cost analysis
- ❌ Learning the workflow and integrating with existing estimating software requires initial setup time

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Togal.AI is the right tool if you:

- **Produce multiple construction bids per week** and want to increase bid volume or reduce estimating hours
- **Have a dedicated estimating team** that spends significant time on manual takeoff from large drawing sets
- **Want to reduce bid preparation errors** introduced by manual counting and measurement
- **Compete for commercial construction work** where drawing sets are large and takeoff is time-intensive

Togal.AI is **not** the right tool if you:

- Bid primarily on small residential projects where manual takeoff is quick and low-risk
- Need full cost estimating software rather than just quantity measurement automation
- Require BIM-based takeoff from 3D models rather than 2D PDF drawings

## Alternatives to Consider

- **PlanSwift** — established digital takeoff tool, less AI-automated but widely used and well-integrated
- **Bluebeam Revu** — industry-standard PDF markup and measurement tool, not AI-powered but highly reliable
- **STACK** — cloud-based takeoff and estimating platform with growing AI features
- **On-Screen Takeoff** — traditional digital takeoff software with a large contractor user base
- **Procore Estimating** — integrated estimating within the broader Procore construction management platform

## Final Verdict

Togal.AI addresses one of construction estimating's most persistent time sinks — manual quantity takeoff — with AI automation that demonstrably reduces the hours required per bid. For estimating teams that produce high bid volumes, the time recovered per bid multiplied by bid count per month makes a clear economic case.

The verification requirement is real — AI measurement is fast but not infallible, and estimators need to check outputs before committing to a bid. But the right model is AI handles the measurement, estimator handles the verification and judgment — which is a better division of labor than estimator handles everything manually.

**Our recommendation:** Run Togal.AI alongside your current takeoff process on the same set of drawings for your next three bids and compare time spent and output accuracy.
