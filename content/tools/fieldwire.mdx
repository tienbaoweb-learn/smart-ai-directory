## Overview

Fieldwire is a construction field management platform built around the day-to-day needs of site teams — distributing drawings, assigning tasks, tracking punch lists, and coordinating inspections from mobile devices on the job site. Its mobile-first design and offline capability make it particularly suited to the realities of construction site work, where internet connectivity is often unreliable and workers need tools that function on a phone or tablet.

Now part of the Hilti Group, Fieldwire is used across general contractors, subcontractors, and specialty trades on commercial construction, residential, and infrastructure projects. It occupies the practical middle ground between basic project management tools and complex enterprise construction platforms like Procore.

This review evaluates Fieldwire based on its field management capabilities, mobile performance, and practical fit for construction site teams in 2026.

---

## Key Features

### Mobile Drawing Access and Markup

Fieldwire distributes the latest drawing revisions to all field devices automatically, with markup tools that allow teams to annotate plans, attach photos, and create task links directly on drawings — ensuring field workers are always working from current information.

### Task and Punch List Management

Task creation, assignment, and tracking is Fieldwire's core workflow. Superintendents can assign tasks to specific trades, set due dates, require photo completion evidence, and track progress from a desktop overview or mobile device. Punch lists follow the same workflow with inspection status tracking.

### Offline Mode

Fieldwire's offline mode caches drawings, tasks, and project data locally so field workers can access and update information in areas without cellular or Wi-Fi coverage. Updates sync when connectivity is restored, which is a practical necessity on many construction sites.

### RFI and Issue Tracking

Beyond tasks, Fieldwire manages RFIs (Requests for Information) and site issues with photo documentation, assignment to responsible parties, and resolution tracking — providing a log of field conditions and design clarifications.

## Pros & Cons

## Pros

- ✅ Mobile-first design works well on site for field workers using phones and tablets
- ✅ Offline mode allows task access and updates in areas with poor or no internet connectivity
- ✅ Drawing markup and version control keeps all field staff on the latest plan revisions
- ✅ Punch list and task management workflows are practical for daily site coordination

## Cons

- ❌ Less comprehensive than enterprise platforms like Procore for financial and contract management
- ❌ Reporting and analytics are more basic compared to larger construction management suites
- ❌ Integration with accounting and ERP systems requires additional configuration
- ❌ Best suited to field coordination — not a full project management solution on its own

---

## Who Is It Best For?

Fieldwire is the right tool if you:

- **Need a practical mobile tool for site teams** rather than a comprehensive project management platform
- **Work on sites with unreliable connectivity** where offline capability is essential
- **Manage field tasks, punch lists, and drawing distribution** as your primary site coordination need
- **Are a subcontractor** who needs drawing access and task tracking without the overhead of an enterprise platform

Fieldwire is **not** the right tool if you:

- Need integrated financials, contract management, and procurement alongside field management
- Require advanced reporting and project analytics across a large project portfolio
- Are looking for a single platform covering all project management functions end to end

---

## Alternatives to Consider

- **Procore** — more comprehensive platform covering financials and contracts alongside field management
- **PlanGrid** — similar drawing distribution and field management with strong Autodesk integration
- **Autodesk Construction Cloud** — broader ecosystem connecting BIM to field management
- **Buildertrend** — better suited for residential and home builder workflows
- **CoConstruct** — purpose-built for custom home builders and remodelers

---

## Final Verdict

Fieldwire is one of the most field-practical construction management tools available — designed for how construction sites actually work rather than how project managers wish they did. The offline capability, mobile drawing access, and task management workflow make it genuinely useful to the people doing the work on site.

For teams needing integrated project financials and comprehensive reporting, Fieldwire's scope is intentionally limited. But for field coordination, punch list management, and drawing distribution, it's among the strongest purpose-built options available.

**Our recommendation:** Pilot Fieldwire on a single project with your site superintendent and lead subcontractors before evaluating it against a more comprehensive platform. The field adoption rate is the most important indicator — tools that field workers actually use every day deliver more value than comprehensive platforms that live on project managers' desks.
